Best days to fly Thanksgiving 2026

The best days to fly Thanksgiving 2026 are an expectation, not a forecast TSA or AAA has published for this year. Thanksgiving Day is Thursday 26 November 2026. The expected outbound peak is Wednesday 25 November, and the expected return peak is Sunday 29 November, with Monday 30 November also expected to be a heavy return day. The lighter day to aim for in the air is Thanksgiving Day itself.

Last updated 13 October 2026.

Editorial guidance only. Peak days below are expectations drawn from the latest official TSA and AAA notes, which cover Thanksgiving 2025. We do not sell flights or lodging, collect payment, or act as merchant of record. See our Disclaimer.

The dates that are fixed

As of 13 October 2026, the U.S. Office of Personnel Management federal holiday schedule lists Thanksgiving Day 2026 as Thursday 26 November. That weekday is the same as Thanksgiving 2025, which OPM lists as Thursday 27 November 2025. The match matters, because the latest official travel notes were written for a Thursday holiday and can be shifted onto the same weekdays in 2026. They still are not a 2026 forecast.

What officials have actually said

As of 13 October 2026, a search of TSA and AAA newsrooms did not turn up a Thanksgiving 2026 forecast. Those notes have landed in mid to late November in recent years. Use the 2025 releases until a 2026 note replaces them.

  • Air, national. On 21 November 2025, TSA said it expected to screen more than 17.8 million people from Tuesday 25 November to Tuesday 2 December 2025, with over 3 million on Sunday 30 November 2025. TSA called that Sunday, the Sunday after Thanksgiving, one of the busiest travel days in its history.
  • Air, returns and the holiday itself. On 17 November 2025, AAA said it is cheaper to fly on Thanksgiving Day itself, and that Sunday and Monday are the busiest return days. AAA also said its booking data put a roundtrip domestic Thanksgiving flight at about $700, similar to the year before. That is a 2025 booking average, not a 2026 fare.
  • Rental cars. In that same 17 November 2025 note, AAA said its rental partner Hertz expected Wednesday to be the busiest vehicle pickup day.
  • Driving. INRIX, quoted by AAA on 17 November 2025, expected Tuesday and Wednesday afternoon to be the most congested periods before Thanksgiving Day, and heavy traffic most of Sunday for people driving home.

AAA also warns that its person trip forecasts and TSA checkpoint counts measure different things. Do not add them together. Checkpoint counts include each time a traveler enters a sterile area. AAA counts a round trip as one person trip.

If you can choose, the practical reading of that pattern is to fly out on Thanksgiving morning, or earlier in the week than Wednesday, and to fly home on Friday 27 November or after Monday 30 November. Friday is not a day TSA or AAA named as a 2026 best day. It is the gap between the holiday and the Sunday and Monday returns they did name. Tuesday 24 November was inside the 2025 TSA screening window, so treat it as busy rather than as a quiet outbound.

Airport specific notes from 2025 do not automatically describe 2026. TSA in New Orleans said its two busiest local days would be the Wednesday before and the Sunday after. TSA in Philadelphia named Tuesday and Wednesday before, plus Sunday. TSA in Miami named the Saturday, Sunday, and Monday after Thanksgiving. Read a fresh airport note if TSA posts one this November.

The worst driving expectation is Wednesday 25 November late morning through evening on the way out, and most of Sunday 29 November on the way home. The best driving expectation is Thanksgiving Day, or an early morning departure on the other days. AAA also said crashes, construction, and weather can erase a quiet window. Its 2025 forecast included a Denver example, Pueblo to Denver on I-25 on Tuesday afternoon. If you are driving into Denver, treat Tuesday 24 November and Wednesday 25 November afternoons as likely busy until a 2026 metro table exists. Flight planning for that city starts at flights to Denver.

Luxury hotel angles in cities we cover

Thanksgiving week is a family holiday that fills small inns before it fills every large hotel. Noravelle Stays does not hold rooms and does not state availability. Use the city page, then the official hotel site.

  • Charleston. A Historic District house or a Mount Pleasant resort is the luxury fork. Small inns tighten first on holiday weeks. Start with Charleston hotels and the Charleston hub, then confirm the visitor calendar at the Charleston Area CVB. Arrival context is on flights to Charleston.
  • Nashville. Sleep in the Gulch or on Music Row if Broadway is a visit rather than a bedroom. Nashville hotels and Visit Music City. Pair the stay with flights to Nashville or New York to Nashville.
  • New Orleans. The Garden District is the quieter holiday house. The French Quarter is the street holiday. New Orleans hotels and New Orleans & Company.
  • Savannah and Austin. Squares and a courtyard, or South Congress and downtown. Savannah hotels and Austin hotels.
  • San Antonio. The River Walk is the festive walk, and the Pearl District is the calmer sleep. King William is an outing, not a hotel on our list. San Antonio hotels and Visit San Antonio. Pair it with Austin hotels when the trip is both Texas cities.
  • Miami and Tampa. AAA’s 17 November 2025 booking list ranked Orlando, Fort Lauderdale, Miami, and Tampa among its top domestic Thanksgiving destinations. That is 2025 demand, not a 2026 ranking. Warm weather bases we edit are Miami hotels and Tampa hotels. The state of the Beach versus Brickell choice is in where to stay in Miami.
  • Jacksonville. A warm Thanksgiving can be a Ponte Vedra or Amelia Island resort, or a downtown river night, and those are different drives from the airport. Jacksonville hotels and the Jacksonville hub.
