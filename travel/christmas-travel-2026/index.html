Christmas travel 2026

By Noravelle Stays Editorial. Last updated 27 Oct 2026.

Christmas travel 2026 stretches through New Year, because Christmas Day is Friday 25 December 2026 and New Year’s Day is Friday 1 January 2027. As of 27 Oct 2026, TSA and AAA had not published a forecast for this season. The expected heavier days are Saturday 19 December and Sunday 20 December, Monday 21 December through Wednesday 23 December, Sunday 27 December, and Sunday 3 January 2027. The holidays themselves are the days to aim for on the road, with the caveat that New Year’s Day can still be a busy day to fly home.

Verified calendar

The U.S. Office of Personnel Management lists Christmas Day 2026 as Friday 25 December, and New Year’s Day 2027 as Friday 1 January. Christmas Eve is therefore Thursday 24 December 2026, and New Year’s Eve is Thursday 31 December 2026. In the prior season, OPM lists Christmas Day 2025 as Thursday 25 December and New Year’s Day 2026 as Thursday 1 January. The weekday shift is one day. That is why a 2025 date should not be copied onto 2026 as if it were the same stamp on the calendar.

What the latest official notes actually measured

On 22 December 2025, TSA said it was ready to screen a projected 44.3 million travelers from Friday 19 December 2025 through Sunday 4 January 2026. It expected the heaviest volume to be about 2.86 million on Sunday 28 December 2025, followed by 19, 20, 21, 27, and 29 December. The busiest day of the prior year, TSA said, was Friday 27 December, just under 2.85 million.

On 10 December 2025, AAA projected 122.4 million Americans traveling at least 50 miles from home from Saturday 20 December 2025 through Thursday 1 January 2026. On air, AAA said the days leading up to Christmas Day were the most expensive, while flying on the holiday itself was cheaper. It also said New Year’s flights were pricey, with many people returning on New Year’s Day or on Sunday 4 January 2026. AAA booking data in that release put a roundtrip domestic ticket at nearly $900, 7 percent higher than the year before. That is a 2025 booking average, not a 2026 fare.

INRIX, in the same AAA release, expected Christmas week to be busier on the roads than New Year’s week. Interstates would be extra congested the weekend before Christmas, on Saturday and Sunday. Friday 26 December 2025, the day after a Thursday Christmas, was also expected to be busy. INRIX said the holidays themselves, Christmas and New Year’s Day, are typically the lightest traffic days, unless a crash or severe weather intervenes. Hertz, via AAA, expected Saturday 20 December 2025 to be the busiest rental pickup day. Orlando, Los Angeles, Atlanta, Miami, and Phoenix led rental demand in that 2025 note.

AAA says not to compare those person trip totals with TSA screening counts. They count different things.

Book by guidance

As of 27 Oct 2026 there is no official book by date for Christmas travel 2026. Christmas Day is 59 days, about eight weeks away. New Year’s Day is a week after that. Check the airline for the specific day, then the official hotel site for the room type, the fees, and the cancellation terms.

AAA published the 2025 end of year forecast on 10 December 2025. TSA published its checkpoint note on 22 December 2025. Recheck both agencies in December 2026 before you treat this page as the last word. The nearly $900 roundtrip figure is AAA’s December 2025 booking average. It is not a 2026 quote, and it is not a luxury hotel rate.

FAQ

When is Christmas travel 2026 busiest?

As of 27 Oct 2026, TSA and AAA had not published a forecast for Christmas 2026. Christmas Day is Friday 25 December 2026 and New Year’s Day is Friday 1 January 2027. The expectation, drawn from December 2025 official notes and shifted because those holidays fell on Thursday, is a heavier outbound period on Saturday 19 December, Sunday 20 December, and Monday 21 through Wednesday 23 December, then heavier return days on Sunday 27 December and Sunday 3 January 2027.

What are the lighter days to travel at Christmas 2026?

AAA booking data published 10 December 2025 said the days leading up to Christmas were the most expensive to fly, and that flying on the holiday itself was cheaper. INRIX said Christmas and New Year’s Day are typically the lightest days on the roads. For 2026 that points to Christmas Eve, Thursday 24 December, Christmas Day, Friday 25 December, New Year’s Eve, Thursday 31 December, and New Year’s Day, Friday 1 January, as the lighter driving days. New Year’s Day can still be a busy air return. This is an expectation, not a 2026 forecast.

When should I book Christmas travel 2026?

AAA published its 2025 end of year forecast on 10 December 2025, and TSA published its holiday note on 22 December 2025, so recheck those pages in December 2026.
