Ryder Cup 2027 Adare Manor hotels

Ryder Cup 2027 Adare Manor hotels are not a list of rooms this site can sell or price. The tournament week at Adare Manor, County Limerick, Ireland, is Monday 13 September to Sunday 19 September 2027. Match days are Friday 17 September to Sunday 19 September 2027. As of 13 October 2026, Adare Manor says it is not taking bookings for an event period it lists as 1 to 30 September 2027. Check the official Ryder Cup and Adare Manor pages for tickets, hospitality, and any stay. Nothing below is an availability claim or a rate.

Last updated 13 October 2026.

Official dates

On 28 May 2025, Ryder Cup Europe announced that Adare Manor will host the matches from 13 to 19 September 2027. The announcement splits the week into four build up days, Monday 13 September to Thursday 16 September, and three competition days, Friday 17 September to Sunday 19 September. Adare Manor’s Ryder Cup page uses the same split. Adare Manor is the host venue. It says it is not the organiser.

The schedule of the week is more specific about spectators. Practice days are Tuesday 14 September to Thursday 16 September: a community day on Tuesday, an all star match on Wednesday, and the Junior Ryder Cup final plus the opening ceremony on Thursday. Match days are Friday 17 September to Sunday 19 September. Monday 13 September is inside the announced week, and it is not described on that schedule as a public practice day. Gate times, the fan village, and other details were still listed as coming later when we read the page on 13 October 2026.

The DP World Tour 2027 schedule lists the Ryder Cup at Adare Manor on 17 to 19 September 2027. That matches the match days, not the full Monday to Sunday week. The course page uses the same competition dates. Ireland.com, the consumer site of Tourism Ireland, also states 13 to 19 September 2027. Use the Ryder Cup schedule page if you need to know which of those days a spectator ticket is meant to cover.

Tickets and hospitality, official channels only

As of 13 October 2026, the Ryder Cup 2027 page said the ballot was closed. The schedule page says you must register a ticketing account to buy tickets or enter a ballot, and that registering does not guarantee a ticket. We do not know, and we will not guess, whether any general admission, hospitality, or package inventory remains. Check the official site.

Adare Manor says it does not sell or distribute tickets. All ticketing is managed by the Ryder Cup.

Hospitality and travel packages have named official routes:

  • Authorised packages. Ryder Cup Europe says no other persons or companies are authorised to sell tickets, Ticket+, Premium Experiences (its hospitality name), or ticket inclusive packages. The names on that page can change. Read the page. Do not buy from a seller who is not on it.
  • Ryder Cup Travel Services, which that page calls the official provider of ticket inclusive packages for Ryder Cup Europe. The provider site Ryder Cup links for this role is rydercuptravelservices.com.
  • Attend, the spectator hub for tickets, transport, accommodation, and the weekly schedule.

We are not listing every tour operator named on the authorised page. A copied list would go stale, and those firms are not Ryder Cup, European Tour, or Adare Manor pages. Start from the authorised packages URL and stay inside the names you see there.

Where to stay

As of 13 October 2026, Adare Manor’s FAQ says the hotel is fully committed during the Ryder Cup and is not taking bookings during an event period it currently lists as 1 September 2027 to 30 September 2027. It also says the public should not book accommodation until they have secured tickets. Both sentences can change. Check the FAQ and adaremanor.com again. We do not claim a room, a rate, or a waitlist.

The official accommodation page, read on 13 October 2026, describes channels rather than prices:

  • An official Ryder Cup campsite, described as limited to ticket holders, with a separate account from the ticketing account.
  • Ryder Cup Travel Services packages on the Oceania Marina, described as a five star cruise ship in the Shannon Estuary, with accommodation, dining, official four day tickets covering Thursday practice and the opening ceremony plus the three match days, and daily transfers to Adare Manor.
  • Ticket inclusive hotel packages from Ryder Cup Travel Services, described as five nights, arriving Wednesday 15 September 2027 and departing Monday 20 September 2027, with a four day ticket from Thursday through Sunday.
  • An official property rental programme, operated by Ryder Cup Travel Services, described as an eight night rental as standard.
  • Glamping, with the page saying further details would be released. Do not treat glamping as on sale until that page says so.

The accommodation page carries a note that arrangements are subject to an event license. A description on that page is not a confirmation that a package can be bought on the day you read this guide. Open the page and stop if the product is not offered there.

Noravelle Stays does not publish a Limerick hotel shortlist for this week. Naming a city hotel and implying it has Ryder Cup availability would be a guess. After you hold an official ticket, use only the accommodation routes above, or a stay you verify yourself, and keep Adare Manor’s “tickets first” caution in mind. Ireland.com describes the manor and the village. It is a tourism portrait, not a reservation system. The US city guides on this site are a different map. They are not substitutes for a County Limerick bed.

Ireland logistics

The Ryder Cup transport page says Adare Manor is reached via four airports: Dublin, Shannon, Kerry, and Cork. It says there is no parking on site, and it tells spectators to use Train and Ride or Park and Ride. Park and Ride is a paid shuttle, booked ahead, with locations to be confirmed to successful ticket buyers. The page’s own approximate car times to a Park and Ride area, as read on 13 October 2026, are about 2 hours 30 minutes to 3 hours from Dublin, 1 hour 30 minutes to 2 hours from Cork, 1 hour 45 minutes to 2 hours 15 minutes from Killarney, 1 hour 30 minutes to 2 hours from Galway, and 4 to 5 hours from Belfast. Shuttle hops from the Park and Ride areas to Adare Manor are listed at about 20 to 30 minutes from the east area and 10 to 20 minutes from the west area. Those are the page’s approximations, not a live traffic promise.

The same page describes a dedicated shuttle train between Limerick Junction and a temporary Adare Manor station, then a short walk to the gates. Its rail table lists Dublin Heuston to Limerick Junction at about 1 hour 30 minutes, Cork Kent to Limerick Junction at about 55 minutes, and Limerick Junction to Adare Manor at about 45 minutes on the dedicated trains. From Limerick Colbert, the prose says a dedicated bus of about 45 minutes, and the table says about 35 to 40 minutes. Quote the page, then recheck it. Regular Irish Rail times, separate from the event shuttle, belong on irishrail.ie.

Shannon is the closest airport the transport page names, and the page does not print a Shannon drive time in that table. Ireland.com’s golf tournaments guide describes Adare Manor as about a 30 minute drive from both Limerick city and Shannon Airport. Use that as Tourism Ireland context for an ordinary day. On event days the Ryder Cup page is the one that says not to drive to the gate. Airport sites for planning the flight, not the last mile, are Shannon Airport and Dublin Airport. Tourism Ireland points US, UK, and European flights toward Dublin and the regional airports, including Shannon and Cork. Fáilte Ireland is the national tourism authority if you need a visitor context page that is not a ticket seller.

FAQ

When is the Ryder Cup at Adare Manor in 2027?

Ryder Cup Europe announced that the 2027 Ryder Cup at Adare Manor, County Limerick, Ireland, runs from Monday 13 September to Sunday 19 September 2027. Build up days are Monday 13 September to Thursday 16 September. Match days are Friday 17 September to Sunday 19 September. The spectator schedule describes practice days as Tuesday 14 September to Thursday 16 September. The DP World Tour schedule lists the competition dates as 17 to 19 September 2027.

Can I book a room at Adare Manor for Ryder Cup 2027?

As of 13 October 2026, Adare Manor’s own Ryder Cup page says the hotel is fully committed during the event and is not taking bookings for an event period it lists as 1 September 2027 to 30 September 2027. Do not treat that sentence as a permanent block or as a price. Check adaremanor.com and the official Ryder Cup accommodation page again before you plan.

Where do official Ryder Cup 2027 tickets and hospitality packages come from?

Tickets are sold through the Ryder Cup, not through Adare Manor. As of 13 October 2026 the Ryder Cup 2027 page said the ballot was closed. Ryder Cup Travel Services is named as the official provider of ticket inclusive packages. Hospitality, called Premium Experiences on the official site, and other ticket inclusive packages may be sold only by parties listed on the Ryder Cup authorised packages page. This guide does not state a price or say that any ticket is still on sale.

How do you get to Adare Manor for the 2027 Ryder Cup?

The Ryder Cup transport page names Dublin, Shannon, Kerry, and Cork airports, says there is no parking on site, and points spectators to Park and Ride or to rail via Limerick Junction and a temporary Adare Manor station. Tourism Ireland’s Ireland.com golf guide describes Adare Manor as about a 30 minute drive from Limerick city and from Shannon Airport. That drive is not a substitute for the event transport plan. Check the official transport page for times.
