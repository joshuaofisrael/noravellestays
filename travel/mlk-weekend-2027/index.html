MLK weekend 2027 luxury long weekend ideas

By Noravelle Stays Editorial. Last updated 27 Oct 2026.

The Birthday of Martin Luther King, Jr. is Monday 18 January 2027 on the federal calendar. For a Monday to Friday schedule, the long weekend is Saturday 16 January through Monday 18 January. This page is the luxury edit for that weekend: cities this site already covers, official tourism boards for the calendar, and book by guidance that does not pretend an agency has set a deadline.

The date that is fixed

The U.S. Office of Personnel Management 2027 holiday schedule lists Monday 18 January as the Birthday of Martin Luther King, Jr. The same table lists Friday 1 January 2027 as New Year's Day. The two holidays are separate trips unless you choose to connect them. OPM does not publish a school calendar, and it does not say that private offices close. If the trip depends on a day off, confirm that with the employer or the school, not with this page.

Saturday 16 January through Monday 18 January is the three night shape of the weekend for someone who works Tuesday 19 January. Friday 15 January is the extra night if Friday is also free. Those are calendar readings of the OPM date. They are not a forecast of airport peaks. TSA and AAA had not published an MLK 2027 travel forecast when we checked on 27 Oct 2026.

Book by guidance

Last updated 27 Oct 2026. No federal travel agency has published a book by date for this weekend. Monday 18 January 2027 is 83 days from this update.

Other holiday notes on this site treat the period from ten weeks out to six weeks out as the window when a specific room type is worth requesting, without calling that window an official deadline. Ten weeks before Monday 18 January 2027 is Monday 9 November 2026. Six weeks before is Monday 7 December 2026. On 27 Oct 2026 the weekend is just outside that ten week mark. The practical step is the same one we would give inside the window: open the official hotel site for Saturday 16 January through Monday 18 January, read the total with fees, and keep a flexible rate if the party size can still change. We do not know which rooms are left, and we do not say that waiting until December is safe.

New Year's Day, Friday 1 January 2027, is 66 days from this update. If the trip is really that week, including the Winter Classic in Salt Lake City on 31 December 2026, use the Christmas and Winter Classic guides instead of stretching this weekend backward. When is MLK weekend 2027?

The U.S. Office of Personnel Management lists the Birthday of Martin Luther King, Jr. on Monday 18 January 2027. For a Monday to Friday schedule, the long weekend around that holiday is Saturday 16 January through Monday 18 January 2027. New Year's Day 2027 is the Friday before that, 1 January.
