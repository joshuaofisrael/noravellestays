Epic Pass price goes up Nov 15: ski pass deadlines and the best luxury ski hotels for 2026 to 2027

By Noravelle Stays Editorial. Last updated 27 Oct 2026.

Epic Pass prices go up on 15 November 2026. As of 27 Oct 2026 the adult Epic Pass is $1,169 and the adult Epic Local Pass is $869. Ikon lists its pass from $1,529 for age 23 and older, and has not posted a final sale date. This guide compares those passes for a luxury ski week, tracks resort opening days from official pages, and names ski in, ski out hotels we could verify. Prices are subject to change. We do not sell passes or rooms.

Epic or Ikon for a luxury ski week

Pick the pass from the mountain, then check the holiday dates that pass blocks. A luxury week usually means five to seven ski days at one resort, with the hotel at the base. That is a different product from a pass built around two days at many resorts.

The Epic Pass product page says unlimited, unrestricted access at Vail, Breckenridge, Park City, and more. Beaver Creek is named on the Epic Local Pass page as a mountain with restricted peak dates, so it is an Epic mountain. Open the resort list on the Epic Pass page before you assume Beaver Creek is unlimited on the full pass. We are not reprinting a day count the full pass page did not print next to the Beaver Creek name.

Epic Local is the wrong default for this holiday week at those mountains. The same Epic Local page says the pass excludes access on 27 November 2026 to 28 November 2026, and on 26 December 2026 to 31 December 2026, at Vail, Beaver Creek, Whistler Blackcomb, Park City, Heavenly, Northstar, Kirkwood, and Stowe. It also lists 16 January 2027, and 13 February 2027 to 14 February 2027. Thanksgiving Friday and Saturday, and the days after Christmas through New Year's Eve, sit inside those blocks. On restricted dates the page says half price pass holder tickets are available at Park City and some other mountains, and an advance lift ticket rate is available at Vail, Beaver Creek, and Whistler Blackcomb. That is a ticket, not included skiing.

The Ikon Pass shop page lists Deer Valley, Aspen Snowmass, and Jackson Hole among its destinations, with a price from $1,529 USD for age 23 and older. The page does not, in the text we read, print a day count next to each of those three names. Confirm the day count on that site before you treat Ikon as a full week at Deer Valley, Aspen, or Jackson Hole. Arapahoe Basin, Copper Mountain, Steamboat, Winter Park, and Solitude are also on the Ikon Pass list we read.

Mountain Collective is two days at each partner resort, plus 50 percent off additional days, with no blackout dates on the homepage description. Some resorts require advance reservations. Aspen Snowmass and Jackson Hole are on the resort list. Deer Valley, Vail, Beaver Creek, and Park City Mountain were not on that list when we read it. Two included days, plus half price extra days, can work for a split trip. It is not the same purchase as seven included days at one hotel door.

A Denver sleep with one mountain day is a different trip again. Use Denver luxury hotels, flights to Denver, and the Denver weekend trip when the base is the city. The airport mountain coach is not a ski in, ski out hotel.

Vail

The Arrabelle at Vail Square, 675 Lionshead Place, Vail, Colorado 81657. The hotel site prints that address. The Vail lodging page describes it as ski in, ski out at the Eagle Bahn Gondola, in Lionshead Village. The hotel site also says a covered escalator delivers guests to the mountain base, with the Eagle Bahn Gondola outside the door.

Four Seasons Resort and Residences Vail is a real Vail Village hotel at One Vail Road. On the page we read, the hotel describes a chalet steps from Gondola One. It does not use the words ski in, ski out. It is not in the list above for that reason. Four Seasons Vail is the page to read if that location is the one you want.

Beaver Creek

The Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch prints its address as 0130 Daybreak Ridge, Avon, Colorado 81620. The overview says guests enjoy ski in, ski out access in winter. Bachelor Gulch is on Beaver Creek ski mountain. It is not a hotel inside Beaver Creek Village. The page says the hotel does not provide an airport shuttle. It lists Eagle County Airport at 28 miles and valet parking at $60 a day. Those fee lines are the hotel's own, and they can change.

The Osprey at Beaver Creek, a RockResort. The Beaver Creek stay page calls it a ski in, ski out hotel at the base of Beaver Creek Mountain and says it is closer to a chairlift than any other lodge in North America. That closest claim is the hotel page's wording, not a measurement we made. The Fireside Grill page, inside the hotel, prints 10 Elk Track, Beaver Creek, Colorado 81620. A Vail Resorts lodging list prints 10 Elk Track Road, Avon, Colorado 81620. Use the stay page when you book, and ask which line the reservation uses. The stay page lists valet parking at $35 per night and says pets are not permitted.

Park City

Park City Mountain's planned opening is 20 November, so this is the Park City base that matches an open mountain at Thanksgiving, if that opening holds.

Park City is the usual add on when the trip starts in Salt Lake City. Use this page for the pass and for mountains outside that add on. Fly into Salt Lake City only after you have read the airline's own schedule.

Thanksgiving week, 26 to 29 November 2026

Last updated 27 Oct 2026.

Thanksgiving Day is Thursday 26 November 2026, and the Sunday return is 29 November 2026, on the U.S. Office of Personnel Management holiday list. The flight and driving peaks for the holiday are on best days to fly Thanksgiving 2026. This section is only about whether the mountain is planned to be open.

  • Likely ski days, if the opening dates hold. Vail is planned to have been open since 13 November. Park City Mountain is planned from 20 November. Beaver Creek is planned from 25 November, the day before Thanksgiving. Use the full Epic Pass if you need 27 and 28 November at Vail, Beaver Creek, or Park City. Epic Local excludes those two dates there.
  • Opening day itself. Aspen Mountain and Snowmass are planned for 26 November, which is Thanksgiving Day. Jackson Hole lists 27 November, the Friday. An opening day is often a limited map. The resort pages we read did not promise a full mountain. Recheck the snow report that week.
  • Not a ski day yet. Deer Valley is slated for 5 December, conditions permitting. Do not book Montage Deer Valley or Stein Eriksen Lodge for Thanksgiving week and expect the lifts outside the door to be running, unless a newer Deer Valley release moves the date.
  • Still unscheduled. Arapahoe Basin and Keystone have not named a numbered opening day. They might be open by Thanksgiving. They might not. We will not guess.

For the hotel, Waldorf Astoria Park City matches a Park City Mountain opening before the holiday. The Arrabelle matches a Vail opening before the holiday. The Osprey and The Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch match a Beaver Creek opening on the day before Thanksgiving. The Little Nell and Viceroy Snowmass match a 26 November opening. None of those sentences is a room hold.

FAQ

When does the Epic Pass price go up in November 2026?

As of 27 Oct 2026, the Epic Pass and Epic Local Pass product pages both say prices go up 15 November. The adult Epic Pass price displayed that day was $1,169 for ages 31 and older. The adult Epic Local Pass price displayed that day was $869 for ages 31 and older. Prices are subject to change. A final sale date for the 2026 to 2027 Epic Pass was not announced on the pages we read.

When is the last day to buy an Epic Pass for 2026 to 2027?

As of 27 Oct 2026, Epic has not announced the last day to buy a 2026 to 2027 pass. Last season, a Vail Resorts release dated 17 November 2025 said Epic Passes went off sale on 4 December 2025. That date is not this season's deadline. The next confirmed price change is 15 November 2026.

When do Ikon Pass sales end for 2026 to 2027?

As of 27 Oct 2026, the Ikon Pass shop page lists the pass from $1,529 USD for age 23 and older, and neither that page nor the Ikon homepage we read printed a final sale date. An upgrade of a pass already purchased must be elected by 10 December 2026. That upgrade date is not a deadline to buy a new pass.

Is Epic or Ikon the better pass for a luxury ski week?

For a full week at Vail or Park City, the Epic Pass product page says unlimited, unrestricted access at Vail, Breckenridge, Park City, and more. Epic Local blocks 27 and 28 November 2026 and 26 to 31 December 2026 at Vail, Beaver Creek, and Park City. Deer Valley, Aspen Snowmass, and Jackson Hole are listed on the Ikon Pass. Confirm the day count for each mountain on ikonpass.com before you buy. Mountain Collective is two days at each partner resort, plus half price additional days, which is a different trip from one resort for a week.

When do the major luxury ski resorts open for 2026 to 2027?

As of 27 Oct 2026, resort or operator pages list these planned opening days: Vail Mountain 13 November, Park City Mountain 20 November, Beaver Creek 25 November, Aspen Mountain and Snowmass 26 November, Jackson Hole 27 November, and Deer Valley 5 December, conditions permitting. Arapahoe Basin says it will open for winter as soon as possible and that there is no official opening day yet. Keystone's own site says opening day as soon as possible, without a numbered date.

Can I ski Thanksgiving week 2026 at these resorts?

Thanksgiving week in this guide is Thursday 26 November through Sunday 29 November 2026. Vail and Park City Mountain are planned to be open before that week. Beaver Creek is planned to open 25 November. Aspen Mountain and Snowmass are planned to open 26 November. Jackson Hole lists 27 November. Deer Valley is slated to open to the public on 5 December, so it is not planned to be open during Thanksgiving week. Epic Local does not include 27 or 28 November at Vail, Beaver Creek, or Park City.

Methodology and limits

Written 27 Oct 2026 by Noravelle Stays. Pass prices and deadlines were read on epicpass.com, ikonpass.com, and mountaincollective.com, plus the Vail Resorts newsroom. Opening days were read on each resort site where a page is linked, and on Vail Resorts releases where the table says so. Hotel names, addresses, and ski access lines were read on the hotel or resort lodging pages linked in the hotel section. We did not invent a price, a date, a hotel, or a room hold. If a fact was not on an official page, it is marked not announced or it is left out.

Refresh plan. From 10 to 14 November 2026, recheck the Epic price on epicpass.com before the 15 November increase, and recheck whether Keystone or Arapahoe Basin has named an opening day. From 1 to 5 December 2026, recheck whether Epic or Ikon has posted a final sale date, and whether Deer Valley's 5 December public opening still holds. Also recheck the Mountain Collective homepage if you are treating the 17 November social note as a price change.
