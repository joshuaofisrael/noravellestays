Super Bowl Valentine's weekend in Los Angeles

By Noravelle Stays Editorial. Last updated 30 Oct 2026.

Sunday 14 February 2027 is Valentine's Day. It is also the date SoFi Stadium lists for Super Bowl LXI, with the time still marked TBA on 30 Oct 2026. Hotels, package prices, and ticket paths are on the Super Bowl LXI 2027 hotels guide. This page is the shape of the weekend for two. It does not book a table or a room.

Editorial guidance only. We do not sell lodging or event tickets, collect payment, or act as merchant of record. See our Disclaimer.

The four days

On Location hotel only packages read for the Super Bowl guide use check in on Friday 12 February 2027 and check out on Monday 15 February 2027. Monday 15 February 2027 is Washington's Birthday on the OPM 2027 holiday schedule. A holiday Monday can change flights. It is not an airline schedule.

  • Friday 12 February. Arrive and stay in the neighborhood you chose. Santa Monica beach hotels are Shutters on the Beach and Hotel Casa del Mar. Beverly Hills hotels are on the Beverly Hills hotels page. The city overview is Los Angeles hotels.
  • Saturday 13 February. Keep the day in that neighborhood. NFL.com, on 9 September 2026, says Studio 61 is at the Hollywood Palladium on Friday and Saturday night and that musical acts would be unveiled in the fall. The article we read on 30 Oct 2026 still did not name the acts.
  • Sunday 14 February. If you are going to the game, do not plan a long dinner that needs a kickoff clock. SoFi Stadium still said Time TBA. If you are watching from the hotel, NFL.com says the broadcast is on ESPN, ABC, and Disney+. A restaurant reservation is between you and the restaurant.
  • Monday 15 February. Checkout on those On Location packages. Read the airline if you fly that morning.

Halftime, the ticket paths, and the hotel prices as listed on 30 Oct 2026 are on the Super Bowl guide, not repeated as a second price list here. Another event weekend on this site is the Winter Classic 2027 guide. The index is the holiday and event travel hub.
