Editorial and sourcing policy

Noravelle Stays publishes independent hotel and trip guides. This page states how we source facts, how we byline pages, and what we never invent. Last updated 19 November 2026.

Who writes these pages

Guides are published under the brand byline Noravelle Stays Editorial. That byline is the desk, not a personal résumé. We do not invent personal credentials for authors. Joshua Israel Ventures LLC operates the site. Operator details appear on the terms, privacy, and disclaimer pages.

Sourcing

  • Hotel names, addresses, fees, and amenity claims come from the hotel’s own pages, or from an official resort or concessioner page when that is the lodging source.
  • Pass prices, sale deadlines, and resort opening days come from the pass issuer or resort operator pages we can open. Dated guides name the day we checked.
  • Every factual claim a reader would rely on links to a named source in the copy or in a sources list at the bottom of the page.
  • We do not invent room rates, inventory, room numbers, views, quotes, or sale dates. If an official page is unclear, we say so and skip the claim.

Independence

Hotel picks and neighborhood notes are independent editorial choices. Hotels named here do not endorse Noravelle Stays. Affiliate compare slots may appear later; when they are live, the affiliate disclosure explains commissions. Commissions do not decide which hotels are named. The primary booking path we point to is the hotel’s official website.

Images

AI generated photographs are labelled in the caption and link to the image policy. Openly licensed city photographs are credited in the caption. We do not use hotel brand photography or commercial stock photos of named properties as if they were our review photos.

Corrections

If a fact is wrong, email joshuaofisrael@gmail.com or use the contact page with the URL, the error, and the fix. We prefer fixing the page over defending a mistake. Material updates change the “last updated” line on the guide.

What this site is not

Noravelle Stays is not a travel agency seller of travel for this MVP, not a merchant of record, and not a party to your hotel contract. Guides are general travel information, not legal, financial, or booking advice. Always confirm rates, fees, and cancel rules on the official site before you pay.
