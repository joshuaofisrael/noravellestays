When is Travel Tuesday 2026?

Thanksgiving Day 2026 is Thursday 26 November. That date is on the U.S. Office of Personnel Management federal holiday list, read on 28 Oct 2026. The days after it are ordinary calendar days with retail names. They are not extra federal holidays.

What Travel Tuesday is

Travel Tuesday is the name attached to that Tuesday, when some travel sellers have used the day for sales in past years. It is not a federal holiday, and it is not an event run by Noravelle Stays. A seller chooses whether to post an offer, which routes or hotels it covers, and when it ends. Two sellers can use the same Tuesday for two different sales, or for none.

Fare baseline checklist

A deal is a comparison. If you do not know the fare for your route before Tuesday 1 December 2026, a sale banner has nothing honest to beat. Write today's fare now. Use the same search on the day. This checklist does not contain a sample number, because a number for someone else's route is not your baseline.

  1. Write the trip the way you will search it: city you leave, city you arrive, date you leave, date you return if you need one, cabin, and number of travelers.
  2. Open the airline site, or the hotel site, that you would actually book. Write the fare you see, the date, and the time.
  3. Write the seller's own label for that fare, including bags, changes, and refunds. Use their words.
  4. Save the note in the tracker on this page, or copy the same fields onto paper if the browser will not store it. The note stays on your device either way. We do not receive it.
  5. On the morning of Tuesday 1 December 2026, search that same trip again before you buy.
  6. Compare the new fare with your note only when the route, the dates, the cabin, the traveler count, and the rules match.
  7. If no announced offer names your route that day, your note is the whole comparison. Do not borrow a discount from a different city.

Book now or wait

There is no announced Travel Tuesday 2026 offer on this page, so waiting is not a plan that rests on a published sale. It is a plan that rests on your own note.

Write today's fare for the trip you actually need. On Tuesday 1 December 2026, search the same cities, the same dates, the same cabin, and the same number of travelers. Read the bag rule and the change rule before you call a lower number a deal. A lower fare that drops a bag, or that you cannot change, can be a worse trip than the fare you already found.

If the seat or the room is one you cannot replace, a sale that has not been announced is not a reason to skip the booking. Read the seller's cancellation rule. A rate you can cancel may let you keep the trip and still look again on 1 December. A rate you cannot cancel is a commitment. We do not know which 2026 sellers will post an offer, or whether any offer will beat the fare you write down today.

    FAQ

    When is Travel Tuesday 2026?

    Travel Tuesday 2026 is Tuesday 1 December 2026. Thanksgiving Day is Thursday 26 November 2026 on the U.S. Office of Personnel Management holiday list. The Friday after Thanksgiving is 27 November, called Black Friday. The Monday after that weekend is 30 November, called Cyber Monday. Travel Tuesday is the next day.

    What is Travel Tuesday?

    Travel Tuesday is a marketing name for the Tuesday after Thanksgiving in the United States. It is not a federal holiday. The Office of Personnel Management lists Thanksgiving Day and does not list Travel Tuesday, Black Friday, or Cyber Monday.

    Are Travel Tuesday 2026 flight deals live?

    No 2026 flight offer is confirmed on this page. Offers are added as they are announced. Record the fare you see today for your own route, then compare that note on Tuesday 1 December 2026. This page does not publish a sample fare.

    Are Travel Tuesday 2026 hotel deals live?

    No. The luxury hotel deal roundup is empty and marked added as announced. Noravelle Stays will not add a hotel name, a discount, or a sample rate until an offer is announced and the terms have been read.

    Should I book now or wait for Travel Tuesday?

    Record today's fare if you can, and read the cancellation rule on any rate you pay now. Waiting helps only if you can search the same trip again on 1 December. A Travel Tuesday sale is not confirmed on this page.

    How do I know a Travel Tuesday fare is a real deal?

    Compare it with the fare you wrote down for the same route, dates, cabin, and number of travelers. Match the bag rule and the change rule. A lower number with tighter rules is not automatically a better fare. If your route is not in an announced offer, your own note is the comparison.
