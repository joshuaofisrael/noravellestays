Flights to Denver

By Noravelle Stays Editorial. Last updated 26 Oct 2026.

Flights to Denver land at Denver International Airport (DEN), well east of downtown. The useful arrival is the airport train to Union Station, then a hotel in LoDo or a ride onward to Cherry Creek or RiNo. This page does not sell tickets and does not show live fares. Flight comparison stays closed. The sleep decision is on luxury hotels near downtown Denver.

What flights to Denver actually mean

DEN is the commercial airport for the city. A nonstop from your origin can change by season, and a connection can add a long sit that the downtown train does not erase. Verify the schedule and the fare on the airline site. Noravelle Stays does not publish a price, a seat map, or a promise that a fare you saw yesterday is still there.

From the airport to a hotel

The hotel guide cites The Crawford Hotel location page for a train ride of about 37 minutes and about 23 miles, with a fare that page prints as $10, and six stops between the airport and Union Station. The same guide notes that the airport transportation page also says 37 minutes to Union Station and trains about every 15 minutes. Those are the pages' planning lines, not a clock we timed on your arrival day. The Crawford Hotel is inside Union Station, so that train is the door. The Oxford Hotel and The Maven Hotel are a short walk from the station. Four Seasons Hotel Denver and The Brown Palace are downtown, a short ride from the platform. Cherry Creek hotels and The Ramble Hotel in RiNo need a longer ride. None of those city hotels print a complimentary airport shuttle on the pages read for the hotel guide.

A car helps with airport luggage, a Cherry Creek base, and a later mountain day. It is a burden if the only plan is to walk LoDo and you do not want to park it. Read the valet line on the hotel you choose. The Denver hotels page records several different parking numbers, and they are not interchangeable. We do not publish a taxi fare here. Use the airport's own ground transportation page the week you fly.

Altitude and the first day

Denver is a mile high city. A same day mountain summit after a sea level flight is a common misfire. Keep the first day lighter, sleep downtown or in Cherry Creek, and put the mountains on a later day. We do not give medical advice. If altitude already limits you, ask your own clinician before you stack a high trail onto the landing day. The Denver weekend trip is the pace note, and the Denver destination hub is the short map.

When flights to Denver get tight

Ski weekends, Broncos weekends, Coors Field weekends, conventions, and holidays move both air and hotel inventory. Book the flight on the airline site. Then lock the neighborhood on Denver hotels. If the ski weekend continues to Vail, Beaver Creek, Park City, Deer Valley, Aspen Snowmass, or Jackson Hole, the pass deadlines and opening days are on the ski pass deadlines guide. If the weekend is the Country Club Plaza rather than the Rockies, start with Kansas City luxury hotels. If you want a shorter Midwest gallery weekend instead of a mountain arrival, compare Columbus luxury hotels. Holiday windows that name Denver sit on Thanksgiving 2026 and Christmas travel 2026.
