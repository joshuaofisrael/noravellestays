Flights to Miami

Miami air travel is frequent from major US hubs — the bigger planning fork is Beach vs. Brickell once you land.

Editorial guidance only. We do not sell tickets or lodging, collect payment, or act as merchant of record. Fares and availability change — verify on the partner site before you book. See our Disclaimer and Affiliate Disclosure.

MIA vs FLL

Miami International (MIA) is closest to many city and Beach itineraries; Fort Lauderdale (FLL) can be a practical alternate depending on fare and ground transfer. Verify totals including transport time, not just the ticket.

Seasonality

Winter leisure peaks, spring break, and holiday weekends tighten seats and Miami Beach rooms. Flexible midweek dates often compare better — check partners rather than trusting any static “cheap” claim on a blog.

Read Miami hotels for neighborhood trade-offs, and watch resort fees when you compare lodging partners.
