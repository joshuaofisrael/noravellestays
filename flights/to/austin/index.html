Flights to Austin

Austin-Bergstrom (AUS) is the main gateway — festival weeks and SXSW reshape both fares and hotel rates.

Editorial guidance only. We do not sell tickets or lodging, collect payment, or act as merchant of record. Fares and availability change — verify on the partner site before you book. See our Disclaimer and Affiliate Disclosure.

Airport & access

AUS sits southeast of downtown. Traffic to Rainey, SoCo, or East Austin varies by time of day; build buffer on Friday festival arrivals.

When fares and rooms move together

SXSW, ACL, and major UT weekends can lift both airfares into AUS and downtown hotel rates. If your dates are inflexible, compare flights early and consider SoCo or East lodging if downtown premiums spike.
