Flights to Nashville land at BNA, and there is no downtown train

By Noravelle Stays Editorial. Last updated 20 November 2026. Refresh owner: Travel site bot.

Flights to Nashville use Nashville International Airport (BNA), southeast of downtown. There is no train from the terminal into the city. The public bus is WeGo Route 18, read 20 November 2026, and rideshare uses the Ground Transportation Center. Noravelle Stays does not sell tickets or show fares. Pair the landing with a neighborhood on the Nashville hotel guide, or with a shuttle hotel on the BNA airport hotel guide when the flight sets the night.

What to do with the landing

BNA is the commercial airport. A nonstop from your origin can change by season, and a connection can land you later than a downtown dinner can absorb. Verify the schedule on the airline site. This page does not publish a fare, a seat map, or a promise that yesterday's price is still there.

The hotel decision is separate from the ticket. A Gulch, downtown, or SoBro night is a walkable music weekend, and that list lives on the where to stay in Nashville guide. A dawn departure is an airport-hotel night. Mixing them, a late Broadway show plus a 6 a.m. boarding pass from a downtown bed, is the mistake this page is for.

The bus downtown

WeGo's airport page, read 20 November 2026, says Route 18 travels between BNA and the Elizabeth Duff Transit Center at WeGo Central, where you can change for other routes. The adult fare printed there is $2 each way. Express trips take about 20 minutes. Local trips take 35 to 45 minutes depending on traffic. Luggage is allowed. Fares may be paid with QuickTicket, a contactless credit or debit card, a mobile wallet, or exact cash. The page says American Express is not accepted, and that a QuickTicket or contactless payment is a 2-hour pass.

At the airport, WeGo's own steps are: leave baggage claim, follow signs to Ground Transportation, find Level 1 of the Terminal 2 Garage, look for the purple WeGo stop, then tap and board. Downtown, the bus is at WeGo Central. The page also says the bus makes other stops, and it points you to the Route 18 schedule, the Transit app, or Google Maps for the stop nearest you. We are not printing a clock from memory. Open the schedule the week you fly.

Rideshare, the curb, and a taxi

The airport's central core page, read 20 November 2026, answers a construction question by saying drop-off and pickup are not moving: passengers continue to be dropped off on Level 3 and picked up on Level 2. The same answer says rideshare, including Uber and Lyft, operates from the Ground Transportation Center on Level 1 of Terminal Garage 2. Follow the Ground Transportation signs rather than guessing a door number from an old blog.

That split matters with luggage. A private-car pickup on Level 2 is not the same curb as an Uber waiting in the garage. If the pin and the door disagree on a busy Saturday, walk to the signed Ground Transportation Center instead of refreshing the app at the wrong level. We do not publish a taxi fare or a traffic time. Both move.

Airport hotel or a city hotel

None of the airport hotels on our guide is inside the terminal, and none of them is a Gulch hotel. The BNA hotel guide, updated 28 October 2026, records the Nashville Airport Marriott overview as listing a complimentary 24-hour shuttle and BNA at 2.3 miles. That figure was not re-read on 20 November 2026. Open the Marriott page before you treat the shuttle as waiting. The same guide is where shuttle hours for the other airport hotels are compared. A departure before a hotel's first run is a rideshare morning, not a shuttle morning.

A music weekend still belongs in the Gulch, downtown, or SoBro. Use the city hotel guide and the Nashville weekend trip. The evening day plan is one Broadway window, not a second hotel list. Noelle's page, as the city guide records it, describes the airport as roughly 8 miles away. Drive time moves with traffic and with event closures. We do not publish a live drive time.

CMA Fest and stadium weekends change the downtown pin more than they change the airport bus. The city guide points at the current festival map because parking near the stadium has been limited while the new stadium is built. Read that map before you rely on an old drop pin. The airport bus still ends at WeGo Central, not at the stadium door.

Questions about the landing

Is there a train from BNA to downtown Nashville?

No. There is no downtown rail from the terminal. WeGo Route 18 is the bus to the Elizabeth Duff Transit Center at WeGo Central.

Where do I catch the bus at BNA?

WeGo says Level 1 of the Terminal 2 Garage, at the Ground Transportation Center, at the purple WeGo stop. Leave baggage claim and follow the Ground Transportation signs.

Where do Uber and Lyft pick up at BNA?

The airport central core page says rideshare operates from the Ground Transportation Center on Level 1 of Terminal Garage 2. The same page says ordinary passenger drop-off stays on Level 3 and pickup stays on Level 2.

Should I sleep at the airport or in the Gulch?

Sleep near BNA when the flight sets the night. Sleep in the Gulch, downtown, or SoBro when the night is the show. Those lists are on two different guides, and a request note will not turn one into the other.
