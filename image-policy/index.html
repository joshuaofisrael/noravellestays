Image policy

This page explains how Noravelle Stays uses pictures. Joshua Israel Ventures LLC publishes the site. Questions about an image can be sent through the contact page. Last updated 19 November 2026.

AI generated photographs

Some photographs and photo-style illustrations on this site are AI generated images. They are not photographs of a named hotel, a real booking desk, or a real guest as they appeared. They are decorative illustrations that sit beside the guides. A caption under each of these images says that it is an AI generated photo and includes a link back to this page.

We do not use hotel brand photography or commercial stock photos of named properties. We do not present a generic room picture as a specific hotel we review.

Openly licensed photographs

City and street photographs on hotel and day-plan pages are openly licensed images, usually from Wikimedia Commons. Those captions name the photographer, the licence, and the file source. The photograph shows the place named in the caption, not a hotel inventory shot.

Credits and questions

If a licensed photograph is added later, its caption will name the source. Treat every photo-style image labelled AI generated as an illustration, and use this page as the source credit for those images. For corrections or questions, use the contact page.
