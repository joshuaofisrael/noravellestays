Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Joshua Israel Ventures LLC (“we,” “us”) handles information in connection with Noravelle Stays at noravellestays.com.

Information you provide

If you use our contact form, we receive the name, email, optional phone number, and message you submit. That data is delivered via FormSubmit (or a similar form relay) to our operator email so we can respond. Do not send payment card numbers or sensitive identity documents through the form.

Cookies and analytics

We may use cookies or similar technologies for basic site function and, in the future, privacy-respecting analytics or advertising measurement. This section is a placeholder: when specific tools are enabled, we intend to update this policy with tool names and purposes. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Affiliate and partner sites

When you leave our site to compare rates on a partner site, that partner’s privacy policy and practices apply to any data you provide there. We do not process lodging payments on Noravelle Stays.

How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries
  • To operate and improve the website
  • To comply with law or protect our rights where required

Retention and security

We retain contact messages as needed to respond and keep ordinary business records. No method of transmission over the internet is fully secure; use care when sending personal information.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Contact

Privacy questions: use our contact page or email the address associated with form submissions (Joshua Israel Ventures LLC).
