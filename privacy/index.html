Joshua Israel Ventures LLC is the data controller for this website. This policy describes only the information the site actually collects. Last updated October 8, 2026.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, your browser sends the form to FormSubmit. The form fields are your name, your email address, your phone number if you choose to provide one, and your message. FormSubmit emails that submission to joshuaofisrael@gmail.com so Joshua Israel Ventures LLC can reply. The message subject is Contact: Noravelle Stays. Do not send payment card numbers or sensitive identity documents through the form.

Cloudflare Web Analytics

This website loads a Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon. That beacon is cookieless. It counts page visits without setting an analytics cookie. This website does not use Google AdSense and it does not place advertising cookies from Google AdSense.

Affiliate links

Affiliate links are not live yet. Affiliate compare is closed, so this website does not send you to an affiliate partner from a compare button today. Affiliate partner cookies may apply only if and when such links are added. If that happens, the affiliate disclosure page will be updated. Hotel buttons on the guides go to official hotel websites. A hotel has its own privacy practices after you leave this website. This website does not process lodging payments.

How information is used

  • To reply to messages sent through the contact form
  • To count page visits with the cookieless Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon
  • To comply with law, or to protect the rights of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC, where required

Retention and security

Contact messages are kept as long as needed to reply and to keep ordinary business records. No method of sending information over the internet is fully secure.

Children
