Privacy Policy

Joshua Israel Ventures LLC is the data controller for this website. This policy describes only the information the site actually collects. Last updated October 27, 2026.

Joshua Israel Ventures LLC is the data controller for the Noravelle Stays website at noravellestays.com. Noravelle Stays is a brand of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC. It is not a separate company and it is not a doing business as name.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, your browser sends the form to FormSubmit. The form fields are your name, your email address, your phone number if you choose to provide one, and your message. FormSubmit emails that submission to joshuaofisrael@gmail.com so Joshua Israel Ventures LLC can reply. The message subject is Contact: Noravelle Stays. Do not send payment card numbers or sensitive identity documents through the form.

Cloudflare Web Analytics

This website loads a Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon. That beacon is cookieless. It counts page visits without setting an analytics cookie. This website does not use Google AdSense and it does not place advertising cookies from Google AdSense.

Google Analytics 4

This website uses Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC) to measure how people use the site. Google Analytics 4 collects measurement data only when you allow analytics storage.

If you choose Accept, Google Analytics 4 may collect the pages you view, the time of the visit, how you arrived (including a referring site), general device and browser details, and approximate location based on your IP address. Google Analytics 4 does not store your full IP address. It may also record interactions on the site that the product measures by default, such as outbound link clicks, scrolls, and file downloads.

When analytics storage is allowed, Google sets first party cookies named _ga and _ga_*. Those cookies distinguish browsers and keep a session. They can stay on your device for up to two years. How Google handles this information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Loading is consent based. Before the Google tag is configured, this site sets analytics_storage, ad_storage, ad_user_data, and ad_personalization to denied. The Google tag loads asynchronously, and analytics cookies are not written while storage stays denied. If you choose Accept, the site updates consent so analytics_storage is granted. If you choose Reject, analytics storage stays denied. This browser remembers the choice in local storage. This site does not use Google AdSense and it does not place Google advertising cookies. Cloudflare Web Analytics, described above, is separate and does not use cookies.

You can opt out with the cookie banner. Choose Reject, or open Cookie choices and choose Reject if you already accepted. You can also use the Google Analytics opt out browser add on at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. Choosing Reject stops further analytics storage. Cookies already saved may remain until you delete them or they expire.

European Economic Area and the United Kingdom

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, this site asks for your consent before Google Analytics 4 stores or reads analytics cookies. Analytics storage stays denied until you choose Accept. You can refuse by choosing Reject. You can withdraw consent later by choosing Reject on the cookie banner. Withdrawal does not undo measurement that already occurred while consent was in place.

California

Joshua Israel Ventures LLC does not sell personal information for money. If you accept Google Analytics 4, measurement data is shared with Google LLC so Google can provide analytics. The California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, lets you opt out of that sharing. Choose Reject on the cookie banner, or use the Google Analytics opt out browser add on linked above. We will not treat you differently for using these choices.
