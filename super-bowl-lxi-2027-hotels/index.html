Super Bowl LXI 2027 Hotels: Where to Stay in Los Angeles

By Noravelle Stays Editorial. Last updated 30 Oct 2026.

Super Bowl LXI is Sunday 14 February 2027 at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood. This guide sorts luxury hotels by Santa Monica, Beverly Hills, West Hollywood, downtown, and LAX, gives drive times as free flow routes or as labelled estimates, and limits tickets to the paths On Location and the NFL Ticket Exchange publish. It does not invent a room, a seat, or a price.

Prices below are the figures on the source pages when they were read on 30 Oct 2026. They are not a promise that the same figure is on the page today.
