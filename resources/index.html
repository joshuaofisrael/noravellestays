Quick luxury trip planning checklist

Printable-style checklist (original Noravelle Stays). Use it before you open partner rate searches.

  • Define trip personality (anniversary beach, design-city weekend, quieter culinary base).
  • Shortlist 1–2 neighborhoods before comparing suite photos.
  • Check official destination calendars for festivals, sports, or major conventions.
  • Budget for resort/destination fees and parking — compare totals on partners.
  • Prefer flexible cancellation during hurricane or severe-weather seasons.
  • Pair flight arrival windows with airport vs. leisure bases (see near-airport guides).
  • Read how pricing works so affiliate vs. merchant roles are clear.
  • When ready, use soft Compare CTAs — bookings happen on partner sites only.
