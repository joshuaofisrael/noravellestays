What Noravelle Stays is

We help travelers shortlist where to stay for boutique and luxury-leaning trips in mid-size US cities, then point you to partner sites to compare hotel rates. Today we operate as an affiliate-only MVP: commissions may come from OTAs and similar partners when links go live. We are not the merchant of record and we do not process payments.

  • Hotels — city and near-airport editorial pages with qualitative guidance
  • Guides — where-to-stay and weekend-trip planning
  • Flights — light route context that pairs with hotel planning

We do not claim to undercut any major OTA’s hotel commission today. Read how pricing works for an honest account of affiliates now and thinner direct-hotel goals later.
