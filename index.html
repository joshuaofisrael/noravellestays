Questions about Noravelle Stays

What is Noravelle Stays?

Noravelle Stays is a curated guide to luxury and boutique US hotels and neighborhood day plans. We name specific hotels and link to their official websites. We do not take your payment and we do not book a stay for you.

How are hotel picks chosen?

Picks are an independent editorial shortlist. The edit starts with the neighborhood, then whether a hotel fits a luxury or boutique stay, with a short reason on the city page. Hotels do not pay to be named and they do not endorse Noravelle Stays. Read how we pick luxury hotels for the full method.

Do the hotel links go to official hotel sites?

Yes. The main link on a hotel pick goes to that hotel's official website, where you check rates and availability. Some pages also link to a city tourism lodging list. We do not take payment on this site.

Are prices on Noravelle Stays live quotes?

No. Prices and fees named on this site are indicative and dated. They are not a live quote, and they can change. Confirm the total on the official hotel site before you book.

Is Noravelle Stays independent, and is affiliate compare active?

The guides are independent editorial. Hotels named here do not endorse Noravelle Stays. Affiliate rate comparison is not active. Compare links stay closed until a program is approved.

How do I contact Noravelle Stays?

Use the contact form at /contact/. It is for questions and corrections. A message cannot book a hotel or process a payment.
