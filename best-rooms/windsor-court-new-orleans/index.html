Best Rooms at The Windsor Court, New Orleans: Which Room to Request

Which room to ask for at The Windsor Court in New Orleans, based only on the hotel's own published room details. Sources were checked on 6 November 2026. See the New Orleans hotel guide for the wider area.

Editorial guidance only. Room requests are never guaranteed and the hotel decides room assignment. We do not sell lodging or collect payment, and the hotel does not endorse Noravelle Stays. Confirm room type, view and price on the official site before you book. See our Disclaimer and Affiliate Disclosure.

Quick answer

Hotel confirms: Deluxe Suites are 600 square feet with a private balcony, while 400 square foot Guest Rooms say only that many include a balcony (rooms page, Guest Rooms page). Club Level is on floors 19 to 22 (rooms page).

Our recommendation: For most couples, book a Deluxe Suite. Book Club Level if lounge breakfast, afternoon tea and evening drinks matter to you.

Ask the hotel: Whether a higher floor, or a room facing the river, is available in the category you booked.

Best floors and views

Hotel confirms: Club Level occupies floors 19 to 22 with skyline views, a dedicated concierge and a Club Level Lounge. The James J. Coleman, Jr. Presidential Suite is on the 22nd floor with views across the Mississippi River and the French Quarter (rooms page). The hotel does not publish which other rooms face the river.

Our recommendation: If the view matters, Club Level is the only floor range the hotel names. Below it, a higher floor is a sensible request, not a published plan.

Ask the hotel: Which side of the building your room faces, and whether a river facing room is open in your category.

Rooms and wings to request

Deluxe Suite

Hotel confirms: 600 square feet, one king bed and a private balcony (rooms page).

Our recommendation: The smallest category the hotel lists with a balcony on every unit.

Club Level, floors 19 to 22

Hotel confirms: Lounge access with breakfast, afternoon tea, evening cocktails and nightly desserts (rooms page).

Our recommendation: Book it for the lounge and the highest floors the hotel names.

Two Bedroom Corner Suite or connecting rooms

Hotel confirms: The corner suite is 1,250 square feet with two baths and a balcony, and many Deluxe and Premium Suites connect to adjoining Guest Rooms or suites (rooms page).

Ask the hotel: Reservations to map a connecting layout for your group.

The hotel does not publish specific room numbers or stacks, so we do not name any. Ask for the room type and outlook instead.

Rooms to avoid and why

A Guest Room, if a balcony is the point

Hotel confirms: Many Guest Rooms include a private balcony, not all (Guest Rooms page).

Our recommendation: Book a Deluxe Suite, or get a balcony confirmed in writing.

Rooms near elevators or service areas

Hotel confirms: The hotel describes double paned windows and a quieter setting just beyond the French Quarter (rooms page). We found no published report of noisy rooms.

Our recommendation: As a general precaution for light sleepers at any hotel, not a finding about this one, ask for a room away from elevators and service areas.

How to ask

  • At booking: book the category that guarantees what you care about (a Deluxe Suite for a balcony, Club Level for floors 19 to 22), then add a note such as "Higher floor, river facing if possible."
  • By phone: the hotel asks guests to call reservations at (888) 596 0955 for specific room inventory and accessibility details (rooms page).
  • Accessibility: ADA rooms include roll in showers, grab bars and visual alerts (rooms page).
  • At check in: ask politely whether a higher floor or river facing room is open in your category before you take the key.

Questions about rooms at The Windsor Court

What is the best room at The Windsor Court?

For most couples, a Deluxe Suite. The hotel lists it at 600 square feet with a private balcony. For a lounge and the highest named floors, book Club Level on floors 19 to 22.

Which floors are Club Level at The Windsor Court?

The hotel says Club Level occupies floors 19 through 22, with a dedicated concierge and a Club Level Lounge.

Do all rooms at The Windsor Court have a balcony?

No. The hotel says many Guest Rooms and many Premium Suites include a private balcony. Deluxe Suites list a private balcony.

Can I request a river view at The Windsor Court?

You can ask, but the hotel does not publish which rooms face the river, apart from the Presidential Suite. A request is not a guarantee, so confirm the view with the hotel if it matters.
