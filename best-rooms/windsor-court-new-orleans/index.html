Best Rooms at The Windsor Court, New Orleans: Which Room to Request

Which room to ask for at The Windsor Court in New Orleans, based only on the hotel's own published room details. Sources were checked on 6 November 2026. See the New Orleans hotel guide for the wider area.

Editorial guidance only. Room requests are never guaranteed and the hotel decides room assignment. We do not sell lodging or collect payment, and the hotel does not endorse Noravelle Stays. Confirm room type, view and price on the official site before you book. See our Disclaimer and Affiliate Disclosure.

Quick answer

For most couples, book a Deluxe Suite and ask for a river view. The hotel lists Deluxe Suites at 600 square feet with a private balcony, while the 400 square foot Guest Rooms only say that many include a balcony. If lounge breakfast, afternoon tea and evening drinks matter, book Club Level, which the hotel places on floors 19 to 22. Families should look at the Two Bedroom Corner Suite or ask for connecting rooms (official rooms page).

Best floors and views

The hotel says Club Level occupies floors 19 to 22, with a dedicated concierge and a Club Level Lounge serving breakfast, afternoon tea, evening cocktails and nightly desserts. The James J. Coleman, Jr. Presidential Suite is on the 22nd floor. The owner, Crow Holdings, states that all 314 rooms and suites have private balconies or bay windows with Mississippi River or city skyline views (Crow Holdings). So the main choice is which side you face: ask for the river side if the view matters most to you.

Below Club Level, the hotel does not publish which floors face which way. A higher floor on the river side is a sensible request, but it is a request, not a published room plan.

Rooms and wings to request

  • Deluxe Suite, river view: the smallest category the hotel lists with a private balcony on every unit.
  • Club Level (floors 19 to 22): for the lounge and the highest floors the hotel names.
  • Two Bedroom Corner Suite: for families or two couples who want one key, two bathrooms and a balcony.
  • Connecting rooms: the hotel says many Deluxe and Premium Suites connect to adjoining Guest Rooms or suites.

The hotel does not publish specific room numbers or stacks, so we do not name any. Ask for the room type and outlook instead.

Rooms to avoid and why

  • A Guest Room if a balcony is the point: the hotel says many Guest Rooms include a balcony, not all. Book a Deluxe Suite or confirm a balcony in writing.
  • A city skyline room if you came for the river: both outlooks exist, so ask for the river side when you book and again at check in.
  • Noise: the hotel describes double paned windows and a quieter setting just beyond the French Quarter, and we found no published list of noisy rooms. If you are a light sleeper, ask for a room away from elevators and service areas.

How to ask

  • At booking: book the category that guarantees what you care about (a Deluxe Suite for a balcony, Club Level for floors 19 to 22). Add a note: "High floor, river view, balcony please."
  • By phone: the hotel asks guests to call reservations at (888) 596 0955 for specific room inventory and accessibility details.
  • Accessibility: ADA rooms include roll in showers, grab bars and visual alerts; tell reservations what you need.
  • At check in: ask politely whether a river view room on a higher floor is open in your category before you take the key.

Questions about rooms at The Windsor Court

What is the best room at The Windsor Court?

For most couples, a Deluxe Suite with a river view. The hotel lists it at 600 square feet with a private balcony. For more space or a lounge, look at Premium Suites or Club Level on floors 19 to 22.

Which floors are Club Level at The Windsor Court?

The hotel says Club Level occupies floors 19 through 22, with a dedicated concierge and a Club Level Lounge.

Do all rooms at The Windsor Court have a balcony?

No. The hotel says many Guest Rooms and many Premium Suites include a private balcony, while Deluxe Suites list a private balcony. The owner says every room has either a balcony or a bay window.

Can I request a river view at The Windsor Court?

Yes, you can ask. Rooms face either the Mississippi River or the city skyline. A request is not a guarantee, so confirm the view in writing if it matters.
