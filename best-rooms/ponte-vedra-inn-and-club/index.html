Best Rooms at Ponte Vedra Inn & Club: Which Room to Request

Which room to ask for at Ponte Vedra Inn & Club in Jacksonville, based only on the hotel's own published room details. Sources were checked on 6 November 2026. See the Jacksonville hotel guide for the wider area.

Editorial guidance only. Room requests are never guaranteed and the hotel decides room assignment. We do not sell lodging or collect payment, and the hotel does not endorse Noravelle Stays. Confirm room type, view and price on the official site before you book. See our Disclaimer and Affiliate Disclosure.

Quick answer

Hotel confirms: Superior Oceanfront rooms are the resort's newest and most popular rooms, with panoramic Atlantic views and an oceanfront balcony or terrace (Superior Oceanfront page). Preferred Beachside rooms have limited or no ocean views (rooms page).

Our recommendation: Book Superior Oceanfront if the ocean view is why you are going. Preferred Oceanfront also faces the ocean and adds a kitchenette.

Ask the hotel: Which building your category is in, before you pay.

Best floors and views

Hotel confirms: The resort has 262 rooms and suites in ten low rise buildings (rooms page). The Penthouse Suite is located high atop the Inn with private elevator access (rooms page). The resort map names buildings such as the Atlantic House and the Historic Inn (resort overview), but the official pages do not say which room types sit in which building.

Our recommendation: In low rise buildings, the category and outlook matter more than the floor. Choose by view first.

Ask the hotel: Which building and outlook your category comes with.

Rooms and wings to request

Superior Oceanfront

Hotel confirms: Panoramic Atlantic views and an oceanfront balcony or terrace (Superior Oceanfront page).

Our recommendation: The room to book for the ocean view.

Preferred Oceanfront

Hotel confirms: Ocean views from the private balcony or patio, a kitchenette and connecting rooms (Preferred Oceanfront page).

Our recommendation: A good choice for families who want the ocean and a kitchenette.

Superior Oceanfront Suite, Oceanfront Suite or Presidential Suite

Hotel confirms: Each lists one king bed and a balcony or terrace; the two Presidential Suites have panoramic Atlantic views (rooms page).

Ask the hotel: For the layout and view of the specific suite, since the resort publishes only short descriptions.

Penthouse Suite

Hotel confirms: High atop the Inn, with private elevator access and views of the ocean and the signature fountain (rooms page).

Our recommendation: For a one of a kind stay, the Penthouse Suite.

The hotel does not publish specific room numbers or stacks, so we do not name any. Ask for the room type and outlook instead.

Rooms to avoid and why

Preferred Beachside, if you want a sea view

Hotel confirms: Limited or no ocean views (rooms page).

Our recommendation: Book an Oceanfront category instead.

Superior Beachside, if you want a straight on view

Hotel confirms: Side views of the Atlantic (rooms page).

Our recommendation: Expect that some of these rooms may see only part of the ocean.

Island View or Historic Inn rooms, for a beach view

Hotel confirms: Island View rooms overlook the lagoons and the 9th Island Hole; Historic Inn rooms look over the resort or lagoon (rooms page).

Historic Inn rooms, for noise sensitive guests

Hotel confirms: These rooms are located above the Great Lounge and lobby (rooms page).

Ask the hotel: If you are sensitive to noise, ask about the exact position of your room before arrival.

How to ask

  • At booking: choose the category that guarantees the view. A request note will not turn a Beachside booking into an Oceanfront room.
  • By phone: resort reservations are listed at (888) 839 9145 (resort overview); ask which building your category is in.
  • Families: Superior Oceanfront and Preferred Oceanfront rooms list connecting rooms (Superior Oceanfront, Preferred Oceanfront); request them when you book.
  • At check in: ask whether a room with a better ocean outlook is open in your category.

Questions about rooms at Ponte Vedra Inn & Club

What is the best room at Ponte Vedra Inn & Club?

For most guests who want the ocean, a Superior Oceanfront room. The resort calls it the newest and most popular room, with panoramic Atlantic views and an oceanfront balcony or terrace.

Which rooms at Ponte Vedra Inn & Club do not have an ocean view?

The resort says Preferred Beachside rooms have limited or no ocean views. Historic Inn rooms face the resort or lagoon, and Island View rooms face the lagoons and golf course. Superior Beachside rooms have side views.

Does Ponte Vedra Inn & Club have connecting rooms?

Yes. The resort lists connecting rooms for Preferred Oceanfront and Superior Oceanfront rooms. Request them when you book.

Where is the Penthouse Suite at Ponte Vedra Inn & Club?

The resort says it is high atop the Inn, with private elevator access and views of the ocean and the signature fountain.
