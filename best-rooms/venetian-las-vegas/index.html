Ask for a Palazzo Premium King: Strip or pool is guaranteed

By Noravelle Stays Editorial. Sources checked 20 November 2026. Refresh owner: Travel site bot.

Which room to ask for at The Venetian Resort is a tower and a category, not a floor number the resort has not printed. Every accommodation is a suite. A Venetian Luxury King is the 650 square foot standard and does not name a view. A Palazzo Premium King is 940 square feet and says every one of those suites has a guaranteed Strip or pool view. Noravelle does not have a Las Vegas hotel city guide. Start from best rooms, or compare the fountain question at Bellagio.

Editorial guidance only. Room requests are never guaranteed and the hotel decides room assignment. We do not sell lodging or collect payment, and the hotel does not endorse Noravelle Stays. Confirm suite type and view on the official site before you book. See our Disclaimer and Affiliate Disclosure.

Quick answer

Hotel confirms: The suites FAQ says view requests for the Strip, the city, the mountains, or the Sphere are not guaranteed and can add a cost at check-in. The Palazzo Premium King page says the opposite for that one category: a guaranteed Strip or pool view. The only floor the suite pages name is the top floor of The Venetian South Tower, for the Skyline Grand and the Luxury Loft King.

Our recommendation: Book the Palazzo Premium King when the window has to be the Strip or the pool, then ask which of those two you were given. Book the Skyline Grand or the Luxury Loft when you want the floor the resort actually names. Do not assume a 650 square foot Luxury King includes the Strip.

Ask the hotel: Strip or pool, before you pay, and whether a South Tower top-floor suite is the product you meant.

The suites FAQ puts standard Luxury suites at 650 to 750 square feet and larger Premium, Grand, or Executive suites at 940 to 3,204 square feet. Those ranges are why a 650 square foot Venetian Luxury King and a 720 square foot Palazzo Luxury King can both be "Luxury" and still not be the same room. The FAQ's definitions: Luxury is the standard suite, Premium is larger with enhanced views or a different layout available, Grand steps up in living space, dining, or bath, and Executive is among the largest. Premier, in that FAQ, means upgraded features, better views, or more space. Prestige means the lounge, not a window.

Best floors and views

Hotel confirms: The FAQ says suites can look at the Las Vegas Strip, the city, the mountains, or the Sphere. You can select a preference online or by calling. Specific views are not guaranteed and are subject to availability and an additional cost at check-in. Two suite pages break that pattern. Palazzo Premium King guarantees Strip or pool. Skyline Grand and Luxury Loft King are on the top floor of The Venetian South Tower. The honeymoon page describes the Skyline Grand as sitting on the resort's highest floors with views of the city. The dedicated suite page is narrower: one top floor, South Tower, and it does not name the Strip.

Our recommendation: Pay for the category that already contains the view. A check-in upsell for a Strip view, on a Luxury King that never promised one, is the expensive version of a request. Pool view on a Premium King satisfies the guarantee and can still disappoint someone who wanted Las Vegas Boulevard.

Ask the hotel: Strip or pool, in writing, on a Premium King. On a South Tower top-floor suite, ask what the arched window faces. The page promises the floor and the glass. It does not promise the Sphere.

Suites and towers to request

Palazzo Premium King

Hotel confirms: 940 square feet, king bed, sofa bed on request, guaranteed Strip or pool view, 130 square foot marble bath with a Roman tub and a separate shower.

Our recommendation: The suite to book when the view is the trip and a Luxury King is too vague.

Ask the hotel: Which of the two guaranteed outlooks is on your confirmation.

Skyline Grand, top floor of the South Tower

Hotel confirms: 1,170 square feet, 15-foot ceilings, floor-to-ceiling arched windows, a private king bedroom, 1.5 bathrooms, a wet bar, and a dining table for four. Located on the top floor of The Venetian South Tower.

Our recommendation: This is the request when "high floor" has to mean a floor the resort named. It is a Venetian tower product, not a Palazzo Premium King.

Luxury Loft King, same top floor, smaller

Hotel confirms: 650 square feet on the top floor of the South Tower, 15-foot ceiling, bed by the window, sweeping views.

Our recommendation: Choose it over a standard Luxury King when the floor matters and you do not need 1,170 square feet. Still ask what the window faces.

Venetian Luxury King, when the suite itself is enough

Hotel confirms: 650 square feet, separate living area, Roman tub, complimentary fridge. No named view.

Our recommendation: A fair standard suite. A poor booking if the Strip is why you picked this resort over a smaller hotel.

The resort does not publish room numbers for these suites, so we do not name any. Reservations are listed at 866.659.9643 on the suite pages. Connecting suites are limited. The FAQ says to call that same reservations number, and that connecting suites depend on availability.

Suites to avoid and why

A Luxury King, if you are picturing the Strip

Hotel confirms: The Venetian page does not name a view. The FAQ says a specific view is not guaranteed on a request.

Our recommendation: Move up to Palazzo Premium King, or to a South Tower top-floor suite, instead of hoping a note on a Luxury King is honored free of charge.

Treating pool view as Strip view

Hotel confirms: The Premium King guarantee is "Strip or pool," not Strip only.

Ask the hotel: For the Strip if the pool is not the picture you wanted. The guarantee is satisfied by either.

Confusing Prestige with a view

Hotel confirms: Prestige is the club lounge: private check-in, continental breakfast, afternoon coffee and tea, evening cocktails, and a concierge. The FAQ says you can book it or ask at the desk, subject to availability and an added cost. It does not say Prestige means a Strip window.

Our recommendation: Buy Prestige for breakfast and the lounge. Buy Premium King for the window. They answer different questions.

A floor number in the Palazzo

Hotel confirms: No Palazzo floor chart on the pages we opened. The named floor is the Venetian South Tower top floor.

Our recommendation: Do not ask for "a high Palazzo floor" as if it were a published plan.

How to ask

  • At booking: pick Palazzo Premium King and say Strip or pool. Or pick Skyline Grand or Luxury Loft King if the South Tower top floor is the point. A view preference on a Luxury King is the request the FAQ says is not guaranteed.
  • By phone: suite reservations are listed at 866.659.9643. Concierge is listed at 866.725.2990. Connecting suites use the reservations number.
  • Who the suite holds: the FAQ says a standard Luxury suite holds a maximum of four guests, two of them included, with a charge for each further guest 13 and older. We are not reprinting that charge. Read it on the FAQ the week you book.
  • Clock: check-in is 3:00 p.m. to 11:59 p.m. Check-out is 11:00 a.m. Earlier arrival and later departure depend on availability and can add a fee. The minimum age to check in is 21, with a government photo ID.
  • House rules that change the room: suites do not come with a coffee maker or a microwave. Pets are not allowed. Service animals trained to perform a task are. All suites are non-smoking. The FAQ says in-suite Wi-Fi is included in the daily resort fee and does not print the fee on that page.
  • At check-in: if you booked Premium King, ask whether the key is Strip or pool before you go up. If you booked Luxury King, do not expect the desk to create a Strip view out of a category that never included one.

Questions about suites at The Venetian

Venetian or Palazzo if I want a view?

Palazzo, and specifically a Premium King, if the view has to be the Strip or the pool. The Venetian Luxury King page does not name a view. The FAQ describes The Venetian as classic Italian grandeur and The Palazzo as modern. Both towers are all-suite and share the resort, including the pools.

Does the resort publish a floor to request?

For two suites. Skyline Grand and Luxury Loft King are on the top floor of The Venetian South Tower. A standard Luxury or Premium suite has no floor number on the pages we opened.

Are Strip views guaranteed?

Not as a general request. The FAQ says a Strip, city, mountain, or Sphere preference is not guaranteed and can cost extra at check-in. The Palazzo Premium King page guarantees Strip or pool for that category only.

What is the difference between Premium and Prestige?

Premium is a larger suite. On the Palazzo Premium King page it includes the view guarantee. Prestige is lounge access: breakfast, afternoon coffee, evening cocktails, private check-in, and a concierge. One is the room. The other is the club.
