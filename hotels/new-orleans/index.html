What to know before you book

What fees should I expect in New Orleans?

Taxes, parking, and destination style fees vary by property. Read the total on the official hotel site. Festival weeks can change both rate and terms.

How do I avoid a noisy room near the Quarter?

Stay off the loudest entertainment blocks if sleep is the luxury. Courtyard suites, higher floors, and Garden District or Warehouse addresses usually rest better than Bourbon adjacent rooms. Ask when you book.

Should I book direct now or wait to compare later?

Book on the official site when the neighborhood and cancellation terms already fit. Compare networks on this site are not live yet. Mardi Gras and Jazz Fest weeks reward earlier holds.

How should I treat cancellation here?

Event weeks often tighten rules. Read the hotel policy before you pay. If your dates sit near a festival, prefer flexibility over a slightly lower prepaid rate.

French Quarter or Garden District?

Stay near the Quarter when walking to landmarks all day is the plan and you can protect sleep. Choose Garden District or Lower Garden District when evenings should stay calmer and the Quarter is a daytime outing.
