Hotels in New Orleans, Louisiana

Neighborhoods at a glance

French Quarter

Iconic and walkable to many sights — also noisy. Not every traveler sleeps well on Bourbon-adjacent blocks.

Warehouse / Arts District

Modern hotels, museums, and restaurants with a shorter hop to the Quarter.

Garden District / Uptown

Streetcar access and leafy streets; better for travelers who want evenings away from the densest crowds.

Central Business District (CBD)

Convention-friendly and often a value relative to Quarter core locations.

Marigny / Bywater

Music and local dining; nightlife energy with a different texture than Bourbon Street.

Practical tips

  • Mardi Gras, Jazz Fest, and Essence weekends require early planning — neighborhoods far from parade routes may still feel the surge.
  • Heat and walking distances are real; prioritize A/C reputation and hydration plans, not just the headline rate.
