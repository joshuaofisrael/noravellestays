Luxury hotels in Los Angeles

By Noravelle Stays Editorial. Last updated 30 Oct 2026.

Los Angeles is a group of bases, not one hotel district. For Super Bowl LXI on Sunday 14 February 2027 at SoFi Stadium, the dated note with package prices, drive times, and ticket paths is the Super Bowl LXI 2027 hotels guide. This page names the hotels and sends you to their own sites. It does not claim a room is open.

Editorial guidance only. We do not sell lodging or event tickets, collect payment, or act as merchant of record. Hotels named here do not endorse Noravelle Stays. See our Disclaimer.

By area

Beverly Hills hotels in this set have their own page: Beverly Hills hotels. A Valentine's weekend that falls on the game is Super Bowl Valentine's weekend in Los Angeles. How a hotel gets onto a list is how we pick luxury hotels.

  • Santa Monica. Shutters on the Beach, 1 Pico Blvd., Santa Monica, CA 90405. Hotel Casa del Mar, 1910 Ocean Way, Santa Monica, CA 90405. NFL.com names both in an On Location beach house note dated 9 September 2026. Package prices for them are not printed on this page.
  • Beverly Hills and the West Hollywood edge. Beverly Hills Marriott, Sofitel Los Angeles at Beverly Hills, and SLS Hotel, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Beverly Hills. The SLS overview says the hotel sits at the intersection of Beverly Hills and West Hollywood. Addresses and the On Location prices we read on 30 Oct 2026 are on the Beverly Hills page and the Super Bowl guide.
  • Downtown. The Biltmore Los Angeles, 506 South Grand Avenue, Los Angeles, CA 90071. The Westin Bonaventure Hotel and Suites, Los Angeles, 404 South Figueroa Street, Los Angeles, CA 90071. This page does not print a downtown package price.
  • LAX. The live hotel page we tried for an airport hotel did not open on 30 Oct 2026, so this page does not name an LAX rate. The Super Bowl guide explains the Century Boulevard drive estimate and why it is labelled an estimate.

Drive times to SoFi Stadium are on the Super Bowl guide. They are free flow routes or editorial ranges. They are not game day promises. Other event guides on this site include the Winter Classic 2027 guide and the holiday and event travel hub.
