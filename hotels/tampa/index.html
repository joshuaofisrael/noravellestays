5 star hotels and luxury hotel rooms in Tampa

By Noravelle Stays Editorial. Last updated 26 October 2026.

A 5 star hotel in Tampa, in the way most travelers use that search, is a full service room with a real downtown or waterfront address. This page does not assign star ratings. Luxury hotel rooms in Tampa in 2026 start on Water Street, on Harbour Island, in Hyde Park, in Ybor City, on Florida Avenue, and at Rocky Point. Open The Tampa EDITION at 500 Channelside Drive or JW Marriott Tampa Water Street at 510 Water Street when the Riverwalk is the night. Open The Westin Tampa Downtown Waterside at 725 South Harbour Island Boulevard when you want a waterfront room, a pool, and the convention center nearby. Open Epicurean Hotel at 1207 South Howard Avenue, or Palihouse Hyde Park Village at 1509 West Swann Avenue. Open Hotel Haya at 1412 East 7th Avenue for Ybor. Open Le Méridien Tampa, The Courthouse, at 601 North Florida Avenue, or Hotel Flor Tampa at 905 North Florida Avenue. Open The CURRENT Hotel at 2545 North Rocky Point Drive when the bay is the point. Choose the neighborhood first. Then open the official hotel site. The short map is the Tampa destination hub.

Best luxury neighborhoods for a splurge

Water Street and Channelside

Water Street Tampa is the new waterfront district beside the Riverwalk, Sparkman Wharf, the Florida Aquarium, and the arena. It is the right luxury core when you want to walk to dinner and you accept that a Lightning night, a cruise morning, and a Riverwalk festival are the soundtrack. It is a weak place to assume Hyde Park hush, and it is a weak place to assume the beach is downstairs. Visit Tampa Bay is the wide visitor map. The hotel pages below are the sleep decision.

The Tampa EDITION, at 500 Channelside Drive, is the design hotel in this set. The hotel's own site says it sits in the 56 acre Water Street neighborhood and has 172 guest rooms and 30 suites, including a penthouse of 2,059 square feet. It names Morris Adjmi with Nichols Brosch Wurst Wolfe and Associates for the architecture, Roman and Williams for the interiors, and Ian Schrager for the creative lead. The Marriott overview is the same hotel, and it is where the fees are printed. Check in is 4:00 p.m. Check out is 11:00 a.m. The minimum age is 18. The hotel is cashless. Housekeeping is listed as twice daily. There is no airport shuttle. Tampa International Airport is listed at 8.0 miles. St. Pete Clearwater International is listed at 17.0 miles. Orlando International is listed at 85.0 miles. Those are the page's figures, not a drive we timed.

EDITION parking is specific, and the two official pages agree on the dollars while they emphasize different lines. The overview lists valet at $58 a day and then says the daily fee excludes tax, with overnight at $58, daytime at $40, and event parking at $60. The getting here page prints the same three numbers plus tax, and adds that guests who spend $60 or more in the food and beverage venues get a 50 percent validation, not on event nights. Read the rate you are about to pay before you assume the validation applies to a concert night. Pets are welcome under a tight rule: one pet, up to 25 pounds, nonrefundable fee of $250 per stay. The same policy says the hotel accepts service animals with certification. That certification line is the hotel's wording. We are not giving a legal opinion on it. Wi Fi is described as complimentary for Marriott Bonvoy members who book direct.

The dining list is long enough to plan a weekend without leaving the block, and it is also a noise map. The overview and the restaurants page name Lilac, Market at EDITION, a coffee bar with La Colombe, the Lobby Bar, Punch Room, Arts Club, Azure on the roof, and a Pool Bar. The overview says Lilac has been a three year recipient of a Michelin star, that Conde Nast Traveler names the property the number 4 best hotel in Florida, and it prints a Forbes four star line. Those are the hotel page's claims. We do not audit awards, and we do not copy a star onto the card below. Azure and the Pool Bar are the roof. A room under that roof is a different booking from a room that only visits it. The overview also says the hotel has a spa and a fitness center. We do not publish a spa price, because the pages we read did not print one.

JW Marriott Tampa Water Street, at 510 Water Street, is the full service neighbor, not a second name for the EDITION. Check in is 4:00 p.m. Check out is 11:00 a.m. The minimum age is 18, which is younger than the 21 year rule at Le Méridien, Epicurean, Hotel Haya, and The CURRENT. Pets are not allowed. There is no airport shuttle. The overview lists Tampa International at 7.0 miles and St. Pete Clearwater at 17.5 miles. EDITION, a few blocks away, lists 8.0 and 17.0. Use both as the hotels' notes. They do not match. Valet is $55 a day. Short term daytime valet is $35. The page also names the Pam Iorio Garage at 0.5 miles for off site parking and says to contact the hotel for self parking options. Electric vehicle charging is listed. Wi Fi is complimentary for Bonvoy members who book direct.

The JW's sixth floor is the amenity floor the overview wants you to picture: two pools, cabanas, Spa by JW with 10 treatment suites, and SiX for dining over the pool. Beacon is the 27th floor lounge, which the page calls downtown's highest public rooftop cocktail bar, and it is cashless. Driftlight Steakhouse and Driftlight Lounge are cashless. Turntable is the grab and go window, with hours that vary with nearby events. JW Market is the cafe. The amenities list prints a club lounge figure of 80.00 and says access is complimentary for Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members, plus one guest. We are not turning 80.00 into a slogan. Ask whether your rate includes the lounge or adds that line. The overview also says guests can use additional dining at the adjacent Tampa Marriott Water Street, and it counts those outlets toward a list of 10. That Marriott is a different hotel. If the spa, the sixth floor pools, and Beacon are the reason you are here, book the JW page and read the address 510 Water Street before you pay. The page says the hotel is beside the Tampa Riverwalk and Benchmark International Arena. A game night is the reason many people choose this door, and it is the reason to ask for a higher floor if sleep matters as much as the walk to the arena.

Hyde Park, SoHo, and Bayshore

South Tampa luxury is three maps that visitors collapse into one word. SoHo is South Howard Avenue, the restaurant street. Hyde Park Village is the shopping enclave around Swann Avenue. Bayshore Boulevard is the waterfront sidewalk that hosts the Gasparilla parade. A hotel on Howard is a dining base. A hotel on Swann is a village base. Neither is a Bayshore balcony. The parade still reaches this side of town because the street closures sit on the boulevard, not because your lobby is the reviewing stand. Epicurean Hotel, at 1207 South Howard Avenue, is the culinary house. The homepage says it has 188 guest rooms and suites and calls itself an AAA Four Diamond hotel. That diamond line is the hotel's claim. We do not audit it, and we do not put it on the card. The hotel is an Autograph Collection property operated with Mainsail. The July 2026 fact sheet on the hotel's own site says a $21 million expansion across Howard added 51 rooms and brought the total to 188, with a private lobby, meeting space, a rooftop terrace, and 91 parking spaces in that addition. It also describes eight historic suites across the avenue, each a one bedroom with a kitchenette and a balcony. The fact sheet says the hotel was the first newly built Autograph Collection property in the United States. That is the sheet's history line.

Epicurean's clock and the car are on the hours and information page, and they do not match the fact sheet's airport sentence. Check in is 4 p.m. Check out is noon. You must be 21. Pets are not allowed. Service animals are the exception the page names. Overnight valet for hotel guests is $25 a night. Daily valet is $10. Valet for patrons of the hotel outlets is complimentary before 7 p.m. and $10 after. Tesla charging is complimentary, and the page says prevailing parking rates still apply. The same FAQ lists bicycle rental at $20. The fact sheet tells you to ask an Epicurean host for a cruiser bike to ride Bayshore, and it calls that sidewalk about four and a half miles. We are not merging $20 and a complimentary sounding host note into one promise. Ask which bicycle you are reserving. The hours page lists Tampa International at 7.4 miles. The fact sheet says the hotel is about 15 minutes from that airport and about 30 minutes from St. Pete Clearwater. A mile marker and a minute estimate are different kinds of claims. We do not publish a live drive time.

The kitchen is the reason to stay here, and the hours page is the reason not to assume every room is open on a Monday. Elevage SoHo Kitchen and Bar serves brunch, a lite bite window, and dinner. On Monday through Friday the page lists brunch 7 a.m. to 2:30 p.m., lite bites until 5 p.m., and dinner 5 p.m. to 10 p.m. Weekend brunch runs until 3 p.m. EDGE, the rooftop cocktail lounge, is open Tuesday through Thursday 5 p.m. to midnight and Friday and Saturday 5 p.m. to 1 a.m. It is closed Sunday and Monday. The same page lists Tuesday 10 November as closed and does not print a year, so confirm that date before you promise someone the roof. Spa Evangeline is open Monday through Thursday 11 a.m. to 5 p.m., Friday 11 a.m. to 6 p.m., Saturday 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., and Sunday 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. The fact sheet names executive chef Jon Atanacio at Elevage, a lobby bar, Chill Bros. as the scoop shop, Bern's Fine Wines and Spirits in the lobby, and the Epicurean Theatre with seating for up to 40. It also places Bern's Steak House at 1208 South Howard Avenue, across the street, closed Monday, open Sunday and Tuesday through Thursday 5 p.m. to 10 p.m., and Friday and Saturday 5 p.m. to 11 p.m. The homepage sells a Bern's steak package. The fact sheet goes on to list awards for the restaurant. Those are the sheet's claims about a neighbor. We do not audit them. The fact sheet also names a Victorian Christmas Stroll in December and Gasparilla in January among area events, without a 2026 or 2027 calendar. Use it as a hint that this street has a December stroll and a January parade, then confirm the date with the organizer.

Palihouse Hyde Park Village, at 1509 West Swann Avenue, is the small house in this set. The Palisociety page says it has 36 oversized guest rooms inside the six blocks of Hyde Park Village, with kitchenettes, and a lobby bar meant for hotel guests and locals. Phone 813 820 1685. Thirty six rooms is a different inventory problem from Epicurean's 188 or a Water Street tower. A Gasparilla weekend or a small wedding can take the useful suite while Howard Avenue still shows space. The page we read on 16 October 2026 did not print a valet rate, a destination fee, a pet fee, a check in time, or a cancellation window. We do not invent them. Read the total on palisociety.com, or call the hotel, before you compare Palihouse with Epicurean's $25 overnight valet. Swann is a walk to shops and restaurants in the village. It is not the South Howard door, and it is not Bayshore. If the lobby bar is the outing, ask which rooms sit over it.

Ybor City

Ybor is the historic district east of downtown, built around cigar factories and 7th Avenue. It is the right luxury core when you want brick streets, a courtyard, and one planned late night. It is a weak place to assume a Water Street spa is in the lobby, and it is a weak place to assume Bayshore quiet. The streetcar is the link west, not a promise that the avenue itself is hushed. Hotel Haya, at 1412 East 7th Avenue, is the pick. The homepage describes a hotel in the old city, and the contact line includes florfina@hotelhaya.com, which matches the restaurant. The rooms page names Flor Fina, a poolside bar, and Cafe Quiquiriqui. The FAQ prints Flor Fina brunch Tuesday through Sunday 9 a.m. to 1 p.m., a dining room Tuesday through Saturday 5 p.m. to 10 p.m., and a bar grid that runs later, including Friday and Saturday until midnight with the kitchen until 11 p.m. The cafe answer says breakfast and lunch 7 a.m. to 2 p.m., with coffee until 4 p.m., and it does not repeat the cafe's name. The pool is open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. We did not find a room count on the pages we read, so we do not publish one. A smaller historic hotel can still sell the courtyard room you wanted on a Saturday.

Hotel Haya's fees are specific, and two of them disagree across pages, which is the reason to read both before you pay. The FAQ says a destination amenity fee of $25 plus tax per room per night covers a morning coffee station, a one time $20 food and beverage credit, high speed Wi Fi, the 24 hour fitness center, pool and courtyard access, priority restaurant reservations, two waters, lifestyle events, local calls, and poolside accessories. The word one time, inside a per night fee, is the hotel's wording. Ask whether the credit is once per stay. Overnight valet is $39 a night with unlimited in and out, at the main entrance on 14th Street, which the FAQ also calls North Avenida Republica de Cuba. The Park and Stay offer describes included valet as a $35 a night value. We are not choosing $39 over $35. The FAQ pet answer says two pets per room at $100 per pet, and it does not print a weight limit. The Park and Stay page says a $75 nonrefundable pet fee, no more than two pets, maximum 30 pounds. Read the rate you are buying. Check in is 4:00 p.m. Check out is noon. The primary guest must be 21, with a photo ID and a card. The FAQ says the hotel will hold an extra $100 a day for incidentals. Flexible reservations can be cancelled without penalty more than 48 hours before the 4:00 p.m. arrival. Inside 48 hours, the penalty is one night of room and tax. Advance purchase rates are excluded. Reservations are held until 2:00 a.m. the next day, and a no show is one night of room and tax. There is no airport shuttle. The FAQ also says a credit card recovery fee of 2.69 percent is charged on each card transaction. That line can change the total more than a small valet gap. Smoking is limited to designated outdoor areas on 7th Avenue and the courtyard. Rooms, the pool deck, and private balconies are non smoking, and the FAQ says there is a smoking penalty without printing the amount.

The same FAQ says the hotel is 1.6 miles from downtown and that the TECO streetcar is complimentary, with stops at the Florida Aquarium, Sparkman Wharf, Amalie Arena, the convention center, and the financial district. HART's current streetcar page also says the line is free. The JW and EDITION pages call the arena Benchmark. Haya's FAQ still says Amalie. Ask which name is current if you are telling a driver where to go. The streetcar is the right tool between Ybor and Water Street. It is not a ride to Epicurean or to Rocky Point. Earplugs are on the in room amenity list, which is a useful hint. A balcony over 7th Avenue is the postcard and the noise. A courtyard room is the other request.

Downtown and the courthouse

Downtown Tampa, along Florida Avenue, is the grid between the Riverwalk and the taller historic blocks. It is the right sleep when you want to walk to the museum, the Straz Center side of town, and a planned Water Street evening without living on Channelside. It is the wrong sleep if you wanted a Hyde Park porch or a Rocky Point sunrise as the view from the bed. Two official hotels on this avenue are easy to confuse because both are historic and both use Florida Avenue. They are not the same building.

Le Méridien Tampa, The Courthouse, at 601 North Florida Avenue, is the 1905 federal building. The overview says the building began as a post office and customs house, tied to the cigar trade, and later served as a courthouse. It describes 130 guest rooms and suites, marble, original oak doors, a gold elevator, and courtrooms reused as event rooms, with 4,800 square feet of meeting space. Sal Rosa is the restaurant. The page says the witness stand became the hostess stand and the juror's bench became seating. There is an outdoor pool on the old wagon court, a 24 hour fitness center, and a sundries shop. Unlock Art, the brand's museum program, partners here with the Tampa Museum of Art. The page says guests get in free with the pass in the room. Check in is 4:00 p.m. Check out is 11:00 a.m. You must be 21. There is no airport shuttle. Tampa International is listed at 7.0 miles. Affari Transportation is listed on request at $25 one way and $40 round trip. Overnight valet is $52. Daytime valet is $20. Dinner parking is $5. Dogs are welcome, up to two per room, up to 40 pounds, at $50 per pet per night. Wi Fi is complimentary for Bonvoy members who book direct. The hotel says it sits adjacent to Sacred Heart Church. A wedding weekend at the church can fill a 130 room house even when a Water Street tower still has space. We do not publish a cancellation window, because the overview we read did not print one.

Hotel Flor Tampa, at 905 North Florida Avenue, is the tall historic tower. The guest rooms page says 210 rooms and suites across 19 floors. Many of the categories it describes are about 400 square feet, with a junior suite at about 600. The history page says the hotel was listed on the National Register of Historic Places on 12 March 1996, and that the City of Tampa designated it a local landmark on 19 March 1996. It says the tower, about 240 feet, was Florida's first skyscraper and stayed the state's tallest until 1966. It says the building was the Floridan, later Hotel Floridan Palace after a 2012 reopening, and that in January 2024 it reopened as Hotel Flor Tampa Downtown, Tapestry Collection by Hilton. Book the Flor name. Do not treat Floridan Palace as a second hotel at a second address. The Dan is the restaurant, described on the homepage FAQ as a Roaring Twenties room with New Orleans and Gulf Coast cooking. Cass Street Coffee runs 6:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. Flor in the Morning is breakfast 7:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m.

Hotel Flor's fees sit on two official pages that agree on the items we compared. The hotel's own FAQ says a daily destination fee of $30 covers premium internet, a daily $15 food and beverage credit in hotel outlets, bicycle rental for two, local shuttle transportation, and two bottled waters a day. Pets: up to two dogs, no more than 50 pounds each, $75 nonrefundable per stay, collected on arrival. Service dogs are welcome on that answer. The Hilton hotel info page lists check in at 4 p.m., check out at 11 a.m., valet at $52 with in and out privileges, and no self parking. It also lists the pet fee at $75 and a 50 pound maximum. The Hilton homepage says a complimentary shuttle serves a three mile radius and that the convention center, the arena, the aquarium, and the port are within about five minutes on that shuttle. It also says an airport shuttle is not available, and a location line says TPA is 10 miles away. Le Méridien, at 601 on the same avenue, lists the airport at 7.0 miles. Use each page's figure for that door. The Hilton homepage spells the nearby performing arts center Stratz. Tampa's venue is the Straz Center. We are noting the spelling on the hotel page, not auditing the sign. We do not publish a cancellation window for Hotel Flor, because the pages we read did not print one we will freeze.

Harbour Island and Davis Islands

The Westin Tampa Downtown Waterside, at 725 South Harbour Island Boulevard, is the Harbour Island hotel in this set. On 26 Oct 2026 the official overview still described an upscale waterfront hotel with an outdoor pool, a fitness studio, and Blue Harbour for dining. The same page says food service at Blue Harbour stops at 10:00 p.m., and it also names 725 South Breakfast and The Marketplace. Check in is 4:00 p.m. Check out is 12:00 p.m. The minimum age is 21. Parking is valet only, $50 a day, and $60 for an oversized vehicle. Dogs only: up to two, up to 50 pounds, a signed waiver, not to be left unattended, with a nonrefundable fee of $150 per stay. The page lists Tampa International Airport at 9.0 miles, St. Pete Clearwater at 19.0 miles, and Orlando International at 86.0 miles, and it says the hotel does not provide a shuttle. It places the Florida Aquarium, Sparkman Wharf, the Tampa Riverwalk, and the convention center nearby, and it still says Amalie Arena. Other hotel pages on this guide say Benchmark. Ask which name is on the sign. The overview does not print a room count, so we do not publish one. The page also uses the shorter name The Westin Tampa Waterside. It is one hotel. Davis Islands is still a residential outing. None of these picks sleep there. Do not book the Westin and expect a Davis Islands porch.

Rocky Point and Westshore

West of downtown, the Westshore business corridor runs along Kennedy Boulevard toward the airport, and Rocky Point is the bayfront just beyond it, on Old Tampa Bay near the Courtney Campbell causeway. This is the right sleep when the flight is early, the view is water, and you accept a ride to Ybor or to SoHo. It is the wrong sleep if the Riverwalk was supposed to be the front door. The CURRENT Hotel, Autograph Collection, at 2545 North Rocky Point Drive, is the bay boutique in this set. The overview calls the setting oceanfront. The water at this address is Tampa Bay, at Rocky Point, near Ben T. Davis Beach. We are not repeating the page's four star line. Check in is 4:00 p.m. Check out is noon. You must be 21. Self parking is $27 a day. Valet is $35 a day. The page says daily rates are subject to change and asks oversized vehicles to call 813 281 2545 ahead. Pets: up to two, up to 40 pounds, nonrefundable fee of $150 per stay, described as covering up to five days. There is no airport shuttle. The overview lists Tampa International at 4.6 miles and, in another line, says the hotel is 5 minutes from the airport. St. Pete Clearwater is listed at 20.0 miles. A mile marker and a five minute claim can both be on the page and still not describe your arrival hour. A notice on the overview, read on 16 October 2026, said the pool would be closed from 8 to 10 December. That is a dated closure, not a permanent fact. Ask again if your stay is that week.

The CURRENT's own dining list is Julian, open daily 6:30 a.m. to 10 p.m. with a menu that changes by meal, Julian Lounge with live music on weekends, Casa Cami for Mexican food on the roof, and a coffee bar pouring Buddy Brew. The opening paragraph spells the Mexican restaurant Casa Cami with an accent. A later heading drops the accent. Confirm the name on the reservation. The page describes a partnership with Graphicstudio at the University of South Florida for art in the hotel. It also calls Raymond James Stadium, International Plaza, and the Straz Center short outings. The stadium is the Bucs note. The Straz Center is a ride, not a walk, from this shore. Wi Fi is complimentary for Bonvoy members who book direct. The fitness center is on the 9th floor on the overview. We do not publish a room count, because the overview we read did not state one we will freeze.

Hotel Alba, at 5303 West Kennedy Boulevard, is open on its own site and on its Hilton Tapestry page. The Hilton page says 222 rooms and 11 floors in the Westshore district, and it gives the airport as both about three miles and about two miles. We are not choosing a distance. The hotel site names The Spaniard, a lobby lounge, and a pool bar, and it frames the design as a nod to Ybor even though the address is Kennedy, not 7th Avenue. We did not put Alba on the pick list. It is a residential Westshore base. If that corridor is the point, open the hotel's site and read the total there. Grand Hyatt Tampa Bay still appears on a Hyatt experiences index under property code TPARW, and older Hyatt fact sheets on assets.hyatt.com place it at 2900 Bayport Drive. Those sheets are not a 2026 fee table. One older sheet mentions a shuttle and a distance that another sheet does not match. The live Hyatt page did not return figures we could read on 16 October 2026. It is not a pick on this page. Open Hyatt yourself before you treat TPARW as tonight's hotel.

Clearwater, St. Pete, and the beach day

Clearwater Beach and St. Petersburg Beach are the Gulf for this map. St. Petersburg's downtown waterfront is a different outing again, across the bay. All three are a day from a Tampa hotel, and they are a poor walking base for Ybor, the Riverwalk, or South Howard. None of the Noravelle picks sleep on the beach or in downtown St. Pete. That is a choice, not a resort we forgot. A beach hotel is a different product: sand at the end of the day, and a car or a scheduled ride back if you also want 7th Avenue. JW lists St. Pete Clearwater International at 17.5 miles and says it does not run a shuttle. EDITION lists 17.0 miles. The CURRENT lists 20.0 miles. Epicurean's fact sheet says about 30 minutes. We do not publish a drive time, a toll, or a beach rate. Summer heat makes the beach practical and a midday Riverwalk harder. If heat already limits your day, ask your own clinician before a long uncovered afternoon. We do not publish a forecast. If the trip is mostly the beach, book the beach on purpose and do not pretend a Channelside balcony is a dune.

Near TPA

Full service hotels inside the Tampa International Airport loop suit a dawn departure or a late landing. They are logistics for the flight, separate from a Water Street, Hyde Park, Ybor, downtown, or Rocky Point splurge. None of the Noravelle picks sleep in the terminal. The CURRENT, at 4.6 miles on its own page, is the closest pick, and it still says it does not run a shuttle. There is no downtown rail. SkyConnect, which the CURRENT overview lists as a nearby station, moves people between the airport's own terminals. It does not replace a ride to 7th Avenue. HART's older streetcar page mixes in a note that Route 30 runs between downtown and the airport, with weekday hours 6 a.m. to 8 p.m. and weekend hours 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. We did not treat that older page as the schedule for 16 October 2026, because the current streetcar service page does not reprint it. Check gohart.org on the day you land. Use a terminal hotel only when the flight sets the night, then move to the neighborhood that fits the rest of the trip.

How to choose

Pick the job of the trip before you compare suite photos. A design waterfront weekend with a roof and a $58 valet wants The Tampa EDITION. A full service Water Street weekend with a spa, two pools, and a 27th floor bar wants the JW, and not the adjacent Tampa Marriott unless that is the hotel you meant. A Harbour Island waterfront room with a pool wants The Westin Tampa Downtown Waterside. A South Howard culinary weekend wants Epicurean. A 36 room village hotel on Swann wants Palihouse. A 7th Avenue courtyard wants Hotel Haya. A 1905 courthouse wants Le Méridien. A tall historic tower on the same avenue, under the Flor name since January 2024, wants Hotel Flor. A bay night near the airport wants The CURRENT. Davis Islands, the beaches, Kennedy Boulevard's Hotel Alba, Grand Hyatt's Bayport address, and the airport terminal are outings or flight nights. They are not missing luxury hotels on this pick list.

Smaller hotels sell out first. Palihouse describes 36 rooms. Le Méridien describes 130. EDITION's site describes 172 rooms and 30 suites. Epicurean's fact sheet and homepage describe 188. Hotel Flor's rooms page describes 210. We are not publishing a room count for Hotel Haya, the JW, or The CURRENT, because the pages we could read did not state one we will freeze. A larger hotel can still show rooms when a 36 room house is gone. The table is an editorial fit guide, not a ranking and not a price list. We do not invent nightly rates. Open the official site named in the last column before you pay. Read how we pick luxury hotels if you want the rules behind that short list, and how pricing works for why the compare buttons on this page stay closed.

Timing, seasonality, and event weekends

Gasparilla is the inventory shock with a map attached. The Gasparilla site lists Children's Gasparilla on 23 January 2027, Gasparilla Pirate Fest on 30 January 2027, and the Outbound Voyage on 27 February 2027. The pirate fest info page says Saturday 30 January 2027 runs from 10:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m., with music along the Riverwalk at Curtis Hixon Waterfront Park and MacDill Park, and it says the 2027 schedule is still TBD. The City of Tampa page is titled for 2027 even though the address still says 2026. It names the same two January dates, says many streets will close, and says more than 300,000 spectators are expected. That expectation is the city's, not a crowd we counted. The Gasparilla homepage says the pirate parade uses Bayshore Boulevard. Hyde Park and SoHo feel the street. Water Street feels the Riverwalk party. Ybor feels the people moving by streetcar. Rocky Point feels it only if you drive in. The 2026 HART patron sheet for the prior festival showed streetcar and bus changes that day. Those hours have passed. Do not reuse them as the 2027 plan. Confirm closures on the city page before you hold a prepaid rate.
