Best luxury & boutique hotels in Miami, Florida

Miami’s high-ticket lodging map splits between Miami Beach icons and design boutiques, Brickell skyline suites, and quieter Coral Gables or Coconut Grove elegance — choose the neighborhood before you fall for a suite photo. Qualitative guidance for travelers planning a high-end trip — we name specific editorial picks and link to official hotel websites.

Best luxury neighborhoods for a splurge

South Beach / Miami Beach luxury

Beach-front icons and boutique design hotels for anniversary trips, Art Basel weeks, and sand-plus-nightlife weekends. Request higher floors or quieter wings when comparing partners.

Mid-Beach & northern Miami Beach

Often more resort-caliber and slightly less frenetic than the densest Ocean Drive blocks — strong for couples who want beach luxury without the loudest nights.

Brickell skyline suites

Design-forward urban luxury and business-premium travel: polished dining, club floors, and a short hop to the beach.

Coral Gables / Coconut Grove

Leafier, quieter luxury for culinary and spa-minded itineraries.

Near MIA (premium full-service)

Logistics for connections — see hotels near Miami Airport. Not a Beach/Brickell leisure substitute.
