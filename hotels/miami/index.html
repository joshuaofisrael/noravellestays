Hotels in Miami, Florida

Miami Beach vs. Brickell vs. Downtown vs. Coral Gables — each serves a different trip. Beach access and nightlife premiums are the big forks. Qualitative guidance for US travelers — compare rates on partner sites when you are ready.

Editorial guidance only. We do not sell lodging, collect payment, or act as merchant of record. Prices and availability change — verify on the partner site before you book. See our Disclaimer and Affiliate Disclosure.

Neighborhoods at a glance

South Beach / Miami Beach

Beach and nightlife first. Spring Break and holiday weekends redefine rates and crowds.

Brickell

Urban skyline stays with dining; beach requires a short trip.

Downtown / Museum Park

Convenient for some cruise and cultural itineraries.

Coral Gables / Coconut Grove

Leafier, less neon — better for slower paces.

Near MIA

Connection-friendly; not a beach vacation base.

Practical tips

  • Resort fees are common — read the total price on partner sites, not just the nightly headline.
  • Hurricane season planning means flexible cancellation policies matter more — verify terms before you pay.
