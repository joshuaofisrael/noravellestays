Beverly Hills hotels

By Noravelle Stays Editorial. Last updated 30 Oct 2026.

Three Beverly Hills hotels are on this page because their own sites publish an address and because On Location listed a hotel only Super Bowl package for each one. The wider Los Angeles note is Los Angeles hotels. Dates, drive times, tickets, and the unit conflict on one price are on the Super Bowl LXI 2027 hotels guide. A name here is not a room.

Editorial guidance only. We do not sell lodging or collect payment. Hotels named here do not endorse Noravelle Stays. See our Disclaimer.

The three hotels

Each On Location reservation page, read on 30 Oct 2026, says the stay is three nights, check in 12 February 2027, check out 15 February 2027, and that no game ticket is included. Each page says the price is per room, not per person. Prices are the figures on those pages that day.

  • Beverly Hills Marriott, 1150 South Beverly Drive, Los Angeles, CA 90035. The On Location page showed $3,000 per room and 9.6 miles from the stadium.
  • Sofitel Los Angeles at Beverly Hills, 8555 Beverly Blvd., Los Angeles, CA 90048. The On Location page showed $3,750 per room and 10.7 miles from the stadium.
  • SLS Hotel, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Beverly Hills, 465 La Cienega Boulevard, Los Angeles, CA 90048. The hotel says it sits at the intersection of Beverly Hills and West Hollywood. The On Location reservation page showed $5,700 per room and 10.2 miles from the stadium. An On Location catalog labels that starting figure per person. The reservation page says per room. Read the page you are paying.

Sunday 14 February 2027 is the Super Bowl date at SoFi Stadium and it is also Valentine's Day. The couple's weekend shape is Super Bowl Valentine's weekend in Los Angeles. Other event notes live on the holiday and event travel hub, including the Winter Classic 2027 guide.
