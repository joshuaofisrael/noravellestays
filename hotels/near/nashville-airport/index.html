Best hotels near Nashville Airport

By Noravelle Stays Editorial. Last updated 28 Oct 2026.

The best hotel near Nashville Airport is a flight decision. None of the hotels below is inside the terminal, and none of them is a Gulch or Broadway hotel. Nashville Airport Marriott is the full service hotel whose overview calls the airport shuttle complimentary and 24 hours, and it lists BNA at 2.3 miles. Other doors print a shorter clock, or they print no clock at all. A music weekend still belongs in the Gulch, downtown, or SoBro, which is the job of the best hotels in Nashville guide and the where to stay in Nashville guide.

On the airport, nearby, or in the city

Nashville International Airport sits southeast of downtown. We did not find a hotel inside the terminal on the airport pages we opened. The useful question is which nearby full service hotel prints a shuttle that covers your flight, and when that is still a worse night than the Gulch.

Nearby full service hotels

The five hotels on this page sit a short ride from the terminal, and they do not agree on the distance. Courtyard by Marriott Nashville Airport lists 1.7 miles. Nashville Airport Marriott lists 2.3 miles. Embassy Suites and DoubleTree each list 2 miles. Sheraton Music City lists 3.0 miles. Those are each hotel's figure. We are not averaging them into one drive time. Only two of the pages we opened print a shuttle clock. The Marriott overview says 24 hours and also says the shuttle is scheduled. Embassy Suites prints 4:00 a.m. to 12:30 a.m. The Sheraton, the DoubleTree location page, and the Courtyard overview say the shuttle is complimentary and do not print the hours. Call before a dawn flight.

In the city, when the city is the point

The Gulch, downtown off Broadway, SoBro, Midtown, and East Nashville are the luxury map. None of these five hotels sleeps there. The Nashville hotel guide names those picks. The where to stay in Nashville guide is the neighborhood fork, and the Nashville weekend trip is the pacing. A first evening can follow the Nashville evening luxury day plan. Use an airport hotel when you will not use the neighborhood, then move. The short map back is the Nashville destination hub. If you are still choosing the arrival, start with flights to Nashville.

How to get between the airport and downtown

WeGo Public Transit says Route 18 travels between the airport and the Elizabeth Duff Transit Center at WeGo Central, where you can change for other routes. The fare is $2 each way for adults. Express trips take about 20 minutes. Local trips take 35 to 45 minutes depending on traffic. Luggage is allowed. At the airport, board at the Ground Transportation Center on Level 1 of the Terminal 2 Garage: leave baggage claim, follow signs to Ground Transportation, and look for the purple WeGo stop. Downtown, catch the bus at WeGo Central. The airport's own FAQ, last modified 16 Apr 2021, also says Route 18 boards at the Ground Transportation Center on the first floor of Terminal Garage 2, and it mentions an information booth from 6:30 a.m. to 11:30 p.m. The garage name on that older FAQ and the name on the current WeGo page are not identical. Follow the signs in the terminal.

The same airport FAQ says hotel shuttles, taxis, and limousines pick up at that Ground Transportation Center. We do not publish a live taxi fare or a live traffic time. Nashville Airport Marriott lists Donelson Station as a nearby train station. That line does not make Donelson a ride from the terminal door. There is no downtown train from the baggage claim.

Nashville Airport Marriott

Nashville Airport Marriott, 2200 Elm Hill Pike, Nashville, Tennessee 37214, is the full service hotel in this set. Phone 615 649 4200. The overview says guests get a complimentary 24 hour airport shuttle. The getting here block lists the shuttle as complimentary and scheduled, and it prints that same phone. Ask the desk to put you on the next run rather than assuming a van is waiting. The hotel lists BNA at 2.3 miles. Check in is 4:00 p.m. Check out is 11:00 a.m. The minimum age is 21. Self parking is $22 a day and $154 a week. Pets are welcome, up to two, 50 pounds each, leashed or crated in public spaces, with a non refundable fee of $100 a stay. The introduction describes high speed Wi Fi. The amenity list says complimentary Wi Fi for Marriott Bonvoy members who book direct. Housekeeping is listed as every other day. The hotel says it does not have electric vehicle charging. Dining names on the page include Two Rivers Cafe, the Atrium Bar and Restaurant, and Fresh Connection. M Club access is for qualifying elite members. This is the airport hotel to open first when the flight could land at any hour. It is still not a Broadway hotel.

Embassy Suites by Hilton Nashville Airport

Embassy Suites by Hilton Nashville Airport, 10 Century Boulevard, Nashville, Tennessee 37214, prints the clearest clock after the Marriott. The location page says the hotel is 2 miles from the airport, 7 miles from downtown, and 5 miles from the Opryland Complex. The complimentary shuttle runs from 4:00 a.m. to 12:30 a.m. daily. A departure before 4:00 a.m. is outside that line. Self parking is $20 a day, gated, with in and out privileges. Valet is not available. The same page lists the Grand Ole Opry at 5 miles, Opry Mills at 5 miles, and 2nd Avenue at 7 miles. The phone printed there is 1 800 362 2779. The location page does not print a check in time or a room count. We are not filling those blanks from memory. A flight inside the printed window is the reason to open this page. A Gulch dinner is not.

Sheraton Music City Nashville Airport

Sheraton Music City Nashville Airport, 777 McGavock Pike, Nashville, Tennessee 37214, lists BNA at 3.0 miles and a complimentary shuttle. Phone 615 885 2200. The page we opened does not print shuttle hours. Confirm the first and last run before a dawn flight. Check in is 3:00 p.m. Check out is 12:00 p.m. The minimum age is 21. The hotel describes an indoor pool, a seasonal outdoor pool, a whirlpool, and 32,000 square feet of event space with 24 function rooms for up to 1,300 guests. Old Hickory Grill and Hill Top Eatery are the dining names. The page says the Hill Top kitchen is open until 11 p.m. One pet is allowed, up to 40 pounds, with a non refundable fee of $100 a stay. The parking block lists self parking at $22 an hour, $22 a day, and $22 a week, and valet at $27 a day. A note says tax is not included. Those hourly and weekly figures do not look like a normal garage tariff sitting next to each other. We are not decoding them. Ask the desk which number is on your car. The amenity list says complimentary Wi Fi for Bonvoy members who book direct.

DoubleTree Suites by Hilton Nashville Airport

DoubleTree Suites by Hilton Hotel Nashville Airport, 2424 Atrium Way, Nashville, Tennessee 37214, says it has 138 suites and lists Nashville International Airport at 2 miles. The location page says the airport shuttle is complimentary and labels it a hotel shuttle. It does not print hours. Do not assume a 24 hour van. Self parking is $20 a day. The same parking block says day parking is $10. Valet is not available. Electric vehicle charging is listed as nearby, at 0.25 miles. The phone on that page is 1 800 222 8733. The location page does not print check in, breakfast, or a pool. Open the hotel site for those lines before you treat them as included. This is a suite option at the 2 mile figure the page prints. It is a flight hotel only after the desk confirms the run that matches your plane.

Courtyard by Marriott Nashville Airport

Courtyard by Marriott Nashville Airport, 2508 Elm Hill Pike, Nashville, Tennessee 37214, lists the shortest airport distance in this set, 1.7 miles. Phone 615 883 9500. The shuttle is complimentary and the getting here block says a reservation is required at that number. The overview we opened does not print the hours, so ask. Check in is 3:00 p.m. Check out is 12:00 p.m. The minimum age is 18. Self parking is $15 a day. A note says $20 a day applies after checkout, up to 14 days. A special offer on the same page describes one night of complimentary parking. An offer is not the standing rate. Ask which one is on your dates. Pets: the hotel information block lists a $20 a night fee and a $100 a stay non refundable fee, one pet, up to 80 pounds. The FAQ restates one pet, 80 pounds, and $100 a stay. We are not merging those into a single promise. The introduction says free high speed Wi Fi. The amenity list says complimentary Wi Fi for Bonvoy members who book direct. The FAQ says the hotel does not offer free breakfast. The Bistro is the restaurant. The Market is described as a 24 hour store. There is a seasonal outdoor pool. A special offer calls the drive downtown very short and does not print minutes. We will not invent them.

What to know before you book

What is the best hotel near Nashville Airport for a flight night?

For a night that exists to catch the flight, start with Nashville Airport Marriott at 2200 Elm Hill Pike. The overview says the airport shuttle is complimentary and 24 hours, and the hotel lists BNA at 2.3 miles. The getting here block says the shuttle is scheduled and prints the hotel phone. Embassy Suites by Hilton Nashville Airport prints a complimentary shuttle from 4:00 a.m. to 12:30 a.m. and lists the airport at 2 miles. Sheraton Music City Nashville Airport lists BNA at 3.0 miles and a complimentary shuttle, and it does not print the hours. DoubleTree Suites by Hilton Nashville Airport lists a complimentary shuttle and 2 miles, and the location page does not print hours. Courtyard by Marriott Nashville Airport lists 1.7 miles and a complimentary shuttle that requires a reservation. None of these is a Gulch or Broadway hotel. Use the Nashville hotel guide for that night.

Should I stay near BNA or in the Gulch or downtown?

Stay near the airport when the flight sets the night and you have read the shuttle window. Stay in the Gulch, downtown, SoBro, or Midtown when the trip is the music and the restaurants. There is no train from the terminal into downtown. WeGo says Route 18 express trips take about 20 minutes and local trips take 35 to 45 minutes, for a $2 fare. Those times are the agency's, and they move with traffic. A Gulch hotel is the wrong default for a dawn departure you will sleep through. An airport hotel is the wrong default for a Broadway weekend. Read where to stay in Nashville before you choose the city night.

How do I get from BNA to downtown without a car?

WeGo Public Transit says Route 18 runs between the airport and the Elizabeth Duff Transit Center at WeGo Central downtown. Board at the Ground Transportation Center on Level 1 of the Terminal 2 Garage. The fare is $2 each way. Express trips take about 20 minutes. Local trips take 35 to 45 minutes depending on traffic. Luggage is allowed. An airport FAQ last modified on 16 Apr 2021 also names Route 18 and places the stop in Terminal Garage 2, with an information booth from 6:30 a.m. to 11:30 p.m. Follow the purple WeGo sign on the day you land. Hotel shuttles use the same Ground Transportation Center. The airport says so.

Which Nashville airport hotels print shuttle hours?

Nashville Airport Marriott's overview says the shuttle is complimentary and 24 hours, and the getting here block says to schedule it at 615 649 4200. Embassy Suites by Hilton Nashville Airport prints 4:00 a.m. to 12:30 a.m. daily on the location page. Sheraton Music City Nashville Airport says the shuttle is complimentary and does not print hours. DoubleTree Suites by Hilton Nashville Airport says the shuttle is complimentary and the location page does not print hours. Courtyard by Marriott Nashville Airport says the shuttle is complimentary and that a reservation is required at 615 883 9500. The overview we opened does not print the hours. A flight outside a printed window needs a confirmation from the desk, not a guess.
