Luxury & full-service hotels near Miami Airport (MIA)

MIA-adjacent full-service hotels excel for international connections and dawn departures. Beach and Brickell remain the splurge bases for leisure.

Editorial guidance only. We do not sell lodging, collect payment, or act as merchant of record. Prices and availability change — verify on the partner site before you book. See our Disclaimer and Affiliate Disclosure.

When an MIA premium hotel is the right call

  • Long-haul arrivals with a same-day outbound or early morning departure
  • Cruise or connection logistics that make Beach traffic a liability
  • Business-premium travelers who need a club lounge more than Ocean Drive

Trade-offs vs. Miami Beach & Brickell

South Beach and Brickell (and Coral Gables for a quieter luxury mood) are where anniversary and design-hotel trips belong. Airport luxury is a logistics tool — compare shuttle quality, suite categories, and total fees on partner sites rather than treating MIA as a vacation neighborhood.

What to verify

  • 24-hour desk and late check-in policies
  • Resort or destination fees on Florida upscale properties
  • Transfer time to Beach or Brickell if you split the stay
