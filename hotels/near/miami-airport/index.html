Best hotels near Miami Airport

By Noravelle Stays Editorial. Last updated 28 Oct 2026.

The best hotel near Miami Airport depends on whether you need to stay inside the terminal. Miami International Airport Hotel is inside Concourse E. The airport FAQ confirms a hotel on level 2. If you want a room outside the building, the closest mile figure we opened is Sheraton Miami Airport at 0.6 miles, with a shuttle the hotel says runs every 15 minutes, all day and all night. South Beach and Brickell are a different product. That night belongs on the Miami luxury hotel guide and the where to stay in Miami guide.

Inside the terminal, nearby, or in the city

Miami International Airport sits west of downtown and the Beach. A causeway is part of any beach morning. A flight night should not pretend otherwise.

Inside the terminal

This is the rare airport in this set with a hotel in the building. The airport FAQ says yes, Hotel MIA is on the 2nd level, and it sends you to MiaHotel.com or 305 871 4100. The hotel site says it is in Concourse E on the 2nd floor, Door 11, in the departures area, and that it is the only hotel inside the terminal. Rooms are described as soundproof. Day use rooms run from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. when they are available. You do not take a shuttle. You also do not get a beach. The hotel's own FAQ says Miami Beach is 10.9 miles via the MacArthur Causeway and about 17 minutes by car, and that downtown is 7.3 miles and about 13 minutes. Those clocks are the hotel's. Cruise mornings, Friday arrivals, and event weeks can take longer. We do not publish a live drive time of our own.

Nearby shuttle hotels

Outside the terminal, the hotels we opened print distances from 0.6 miles to 4 miles, and they do not share one shuttle clock. Sheraton says every 15 minutes, 24 hours, from the arrivals level. EVEN says a 24 hour shuttle from arrivals level 1 and about 1 mile. Marriott says every 15 minutes from 5 a.m. to 1 a.m. and 1.9 miles. Pullman says 4:30 a.m. to midnight and about 3.3 miles. Hilton Miami Airport Blue Lagoon says every 30 minutes from 5:00 a.m. to 12:30 a.m. and 4 miles. A flight in the gap needs a different door, a taxi, or a rideshare. The airport's own pages also disagree about which level the vans use. Read the hotel you booked.

In the city, when the city is the point

South Beach, Mid Beach, Brickell, Coconut Grove, Coral Gables, and Key Biscayne are the luxury map. None of the six hotels on this page sleeps there. The Miami luxury hotel guide names those picks and records what each official site says about the airport. The where to stay in Miami guide is the neighborhood fork. A beach day can follow the Miami Beach luxury day plan. Use an airport hotel when you will barely see the sand, then move. The short map back is the Miami destination hub. Pair the flight with flights to Miami when the arrival day is still open.

How to get between the airport, Brickell, and the Beach

The airport public transportation page says the free MIA Mover is on the 3rd level between the Dolphin and Flamingo garages. It links the terminal to the rental car center. Metrobus, Metrorail, and Tri Rail connections are at the Miami Intermodal Center, and you buy an EASY Card there. An Orange Line train is the one the page names for Dadeland, Coconut Grove, Vizcaya, Brickell, Government Center, and stations farther north. The page prints $2.25 each way in that section. That is a mainland train. It does not cross onto Miami Beach.

The beach ride on the same page is a bus. It runs every 30 minutes from 6 a.m. to 11:40 p.m., seven days a week, between the airport Metrorail station and Miami Beach from 41st Street to South Pointe. The station is a short MIA Mover ride from the terminal, not a door at baggage claim. The page prints $2.25 each way for that bus and says the buses have luggage racks. There is still no rail across the causeway. Ground transportation says PortMiami is about 9 miles from the airport. That is a distance, not a minute count. A Sheraton sentence that says the port is just 20 minutes away is the hotel's claim, not the airport's.

The airport FAQ says taxis are on the arrivals level outside baggage claim, that hotel shuttles are on the departure level, and that ground transportation runs 24 hours. The shuttle directory says hotel shuttles pick up on the arrivals level at a list of doors. We are not choosing which airport page is current. Follow the hotel you booked, and ignore ride offers from people inside the terminal. The city hotel guide has the longer note on ride apps.

Miami International Airport Hotel

Miami International Airport Hotel prints the address as Le Jeune Road, Concourse E, 2nd floor, NW 20th Street, Miami, Florida 33122. Phone 305 871 4100. The hotel says it is the only hotel inside the terminal, on the departures level, Door 11, with direct access to ticket counters. It describes soundproof guest rooms, complimentary Wi Fi, and on site dining that includes Air Margaritaville. Day use rooms are available from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. based on availability. A $100 security deposit is required. The hotel says the deposit is released on checkout day, and that fund holds are released at midnight that day. The Beach and downtown times above are this hotel's FAQ, not a traffic report. The page we opened does not print a check in time. Read that clock before you pay. This is the right hotel when leaving the building is the problem. It is the wrong hotel when the Atlantic is the reason for the trip.

Sheraton Miami Airport Hotel and Executive Meeting Center

Sheraton Miami Airport Hotel & Executive Meeting Center, 3900 NW 21st Street, Miami, Florida 33142, lists MIA at 0.6 miles. Phone 305 871 3800. The complimentary shuttle runs every 15 minutes, 24 hours. After you land, the page says to use the lower level arrivals and baggage claim and follow signs for hotel shuttles. Check in is 3:00 p.m. Check out is 11:00 a.m. The minimum age is 21. One dog is allowed, up to 75 pounds, with a non refundable fee of $150 a stay. Self parking is $31 a day. Valet is $36 a day. The page says self parking fees are per night. The introduction says two restaurants and free Wi Fi. The amenity list says three restaurants, and complimentary Wi Fi for Marriott Bonvoy members who book direct. The page names The Rickenbacker Restaurant, River's End Patio, and MbarK. There is a heated outdoor pool and a fitness center the hotel calls 24 hour. The introduction says PortMiami is just 20 minutes away. The airport says the port is about 9 miles. Use both as what they are. Housekeeping is listed as daily. This is the nearby hotel to open when you want a van at any hour and you accept an airport corridor rather than a beach.

Miami Airport Marriott

Miami Airport Marriott, 1201 NW Le Jeune Road, Building A, Miami, Florida 33126, lists MIA at 1.9 miles. Phone 305 649 5000. The page says the free shuttle runs every 15 minutes from 5 a.m. to 1 a.m., with pickups on the arrivals level outside baggage claim, at doors 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 15, 20, 23, 24, and 28. A flight between 1 a.m. and 5 a.m. is outside that line. Check in is 3:00 p.m. Check out is 12:00 p.m. The minimum age is 18. The hotel describes itself as connected with Courtyard Miami Airport and Residence Inn Miami Airport. Self parking is $5 an hour and $28 a day. Valet is $32 a day. A note says parking rates include tax, and that Champions Restaurant validation covers 4 hours. Pets are welcome, up to two, 50 pounds each, with a non refundable fee of $150 a stay in the hotel information block. The introduction describes high speed Wi Fi. The amenity list says complimentary Wi Fi for Bonvoy members who book direct. There is an outdoor pool, a fitness center open 24 hours, and more than 20,000 square feet of event space. Dining names include Cane Fire Grille and MIA's Pantry. This is a strong flight hotel inside the printed window. It is not a Collins Avenue hotel.

EVEN Hotel Miami Airport

EVEN Hotel Miami Airport, 3499 NW 25th Street, Miami, Florida 33142, says the airport is about 1 mile, which it also prints as 1.61 kilometers. Phone 305 636 7000. The complimentary shuttle is described as 24 hours, from arrivals level 1, and the page says to call the hotel for pickup. Check in is 3:00 p.m. Check out is 11:00 a.m. The minimum age is 21. On site parking is $19 a day. Pets are welcome, two per room, with a combined weight limit of 30 pounds and a fee of $75 per stay. There is a pool. The airport shuttle directory lists this hotel at the same street and then prints ZIP code 33180. The hotel site prints 33142. We are not choosing the ZIP. Use the hotel's own page for the booking, and ask if a car service is using the directory address. A 24 hour shuttle at about 1 mile is the reason to open it. A beach morning is not.

Pullman Miami Airport

Pullman Miami Airport prints 5800 Waterford District Drive, Miami 33126. Phone 305 264 4888. The Accor page says MIA is about 5.3 kilometers, which it also prints as 3.3 miles, and that a complimentary shuttle runs from 4:30 a.m. to midnight. Parking is not included. The page says on site parking is an additional fee and available 24 hours, and it does not print the dollar. Miami Airport Station is listed at about 5.6 kilometers, or 3.5 miles. The page describes a lagoon side pool. The airport shuttle directory lists Pullman Miami Airport Hotel at 5800 Blue Lagoon Drive, not Waterford District Drive. We are not merging those streets. Book from the Accor address and ask the hotel which driveway the van uses. The Accor heading calls this a luxury hotel near MIA. Read that as the brand's line. The stay is a Waterford business district hotel with a lagoon, not a South Beach resort. The page we opened does not print a check in time.

Hilton Miami Airport Blue Lagoon

Hilton Miami Airport Blue Lagoon prints 5101 Blue Lagoon Drive, Miami, Florida 33126 on its location page. That page lists MIA at 4 miles and a complimentary shuttle every 30 minutes from 5:00 a.m. to 12:30 a.m., with pickup and drop off on the departures level at the hotel shuttle zone signs. Self parking is $38 a day. Valet is not available. The page says Hertz rental is on site. The phone printed there is 1 800 445 8667. Miracle Mile is listed at 4.70 miles and LoanDepot Park at 5.50 miles. The location page does not print a check in time. The airport shuttle directory lists Hilton Miami Airport Blue Lagoon at 5101 Waterford District Drive, not Blue Lagoon Drive. Same number, different street, same ZIP. Ask the hotel which line a driver should use. A 4 mile shuttle that stops at 12:30 a.m. is a real flight hotel inside that window. It is a long way from a dune.

What to know before you book

What is the best hotel near Miami Airport for a flight night?

If you do not want to leave the terminal, Miami International Airport Hotel is the hotel inside Concourse E on the 2nd floor. The airport FAQ confirms a hotel on level 2 and points to MiaHotel.com and 305 871 4100. The hotel says it is the only hotel inside the terminal and that rooms have soundproof windows. If you want a shuttle hotel outside the building, Sheraton Miami Airport lists MIA at 0.6 miles and a complimentary shuttle every 15 minutes, 24 hours, from the arrivals level. Miami Airport Marriott lists 1.9 miles and a shuttle every 15 minutes from 5 a.m. to 1 a.m. EVEN Hotel Miami Airport says the shuttle is 24 hours and lists the airport at about 1 mile. A beach or Brickell weekend is a different night. Use the Miami hotel guide for that stay.

Should I stay inside MIA, nearby, or on the Beach?

Stay inside the terminal when the connection or the dawn departure is the whole trip. Stay at a nearby shuttle hotel when you want a pool or a quieter room and you have read the shuttle clock, including the gaps after midnight at the Marriott and the Hilton Blue Lagoon. Stay in South Beach, Mid Beach, Brickell, Coconut Grove, or Coral Gables when the city is the point. There is no rail across the causeway onto Miami Beach. Metrorail's Orange Line can reach Brickell and Coconut Grove after the free MIA Mover. The beach bus the airport describes leaves from the Metrorail station, not from the baggage carousel. The Miami hotel's own FAQ says Miami Beach is 10.9 miles and about 17 minutes, and downtown is 7.3 miles and about 13 minutes. Those are the hotel's figures, not a Friday night promise.

How do I get from MIA to Brickell or the Beach without a car?

The airport public transportation page says the free MIA Mover leaves from the 3rd level between the Dolphin and Flamingo garages and links the terminal to the rental car center and the Miami Intermodal Center. Metrobus, Metrorail, and Tri Rail connections are there. Board an Orange Line train for Brickell, Government Center, and Coconut Grove, among other stations. The page prints $2.25 each way in that rail section. A separate bus runs every 30 minutes from 6 a.m. to 11:40 p.m., seven days, between the airport Metrorail station and Miami Beach from 41st Street to South Pointe, and the page prints $2.25 each way for that ride too. You need an EASY Card. Taxis are on the arrivals level outside baggage claim. Hotel shuttle instructions disagree across airport pages, so follow the hotel you booked.

Where do hotel shuttles pick up at MIA?

The airport FAQ says hotel shuttles are on the departure level, level 2. The airport shuttle directory says hotel shuttles pick up on the arrivals level, level 1, at listed doors. Sheraton Miami Airport says the lower level arrivals area, every 15 minutes, 24 hours. Miami Airport Marriott says arrivals level, every 15 minutes from 5 a.m. to 1 a.m., at doors the page lists. Hilton Miami Airport Blue Lagoon says the departures level, every 30 minutes from 5:00 a.m. to 12:30 a.m. EVEN Hotel says arrivals level 1, 24 hours, and says to call the hotel. Pullman Miami Airport prints 4:30 a.m. to midnight and does not print the door on the Accor page we opened. Use the instruction on the hotel you booked, and ask if the two airport pages still disagree.
