Best hotels near Austin Airport

By Noravelle Stays Editorial. Last updated 28 Oct 2026.

The best hotel near Austin Airport is the one that matches the flight, not the one with the best South Congress photo. Hilton Austin Airport is the full service hotel on the airport grounds. Its location page says so, and it says the complimentary shuttle runs 24 hours to the main terminal. Nearby hotels on Ben White Boulevard can be a better room if you have read the shuttle clock. A luxury weekend still belongs in South Congress, on Lady Bird Lake, or downtown, which is the job of the best luxury hotels in Austin guide and the where to stay in Austin guide.

On the airport, nearby, or in the city

Austin Bergstrom International Airport sits southeast of downtown. There is no train from the terminal into the city. That single fact decides most luxury trips. If you want to walk to dinner, sleep in the neighborhood the where to stay in Austin guide describes, then ride to the airport in the morning. If the landing is late or the departure is at dawn, sleep where the shuttle or the rideshare is short and move the next day. An airport corridor is logistics. It is not a second South Congress.

On the airport grounds

The Hilton location page, read on 28 Oct 2026, says Hilton Austin Airport at 9515 Hotel Drive is the only full service hotel on the airport grounds. It lists Austin Bergstrom at 0 miles and a complimentary shuttle 24 hours to the main terminal only. It lists the city center at 7 miles. That 7 mile figure is the hotel's, not a drive time. We do not publish a minute count for that hop because the page does not print one. Self parking is $15 a day. Valet is not available. The location page does not print a check in or check out time, so read that clock on the page you pay. The reservations line on that page is 1 800 445 8667.

Nearby, with or without a shuttle

East Ben White Boulevard holds two shuttle hotels we opened. Holiday Inn Austin Airport says it is two miles from the airport, off Interstate 35 and Highway 71, with a complimentary 24 hour shuttle. Courtyard by Marriott Austin Airport lists the airport at 2.0 miles and also says the hotel is 7 minutes away. Those are both the hotel's figures. A Friday festival arrival can take longer than 7 minutes. The Courtyard shuttle window is 4:00 a.m. to midnight, so a flight in the small hours is outside the line the page prints. Staybridge Suites Austin Airport, at 1611 Airport Commerce Drive, is the nearby all suite hotel whose directions page says an airport shuttle is not available. It is a real stay for a longer visit or a Circuit of the Americas weekend. The hotel says it is less than 10 miles from that circuit. It does not print an airport mile count. You get there by car or rideshare, not by a hotel van.

In the city, when the city is the point

South Congress, Lady Bird Lake, Rainey Street, downtown, East Austin, and the Hancock estate are the luxury map. None of the four hotels on this page sleeps there. The Austin luxury hotel guide names those picks and records what each official site says about airport shuttles. Austin Proper describes the drive as about 15 to 20 minutes and says it does not run a shuttle. Hotel Van Zandt also says it does not run a shuttle. Those minutes are a downtown hotel's note. They are not a promise for a Ben White Boulevard door, and they are not a Friday night during SXSW or a race weekend. If the trip is the city, start with where to stay in Austin and the Austin weekend trip, then use an airport hotel only for the flight night. The short map back is the Austin destination hub.

How to get between the airport and the city

CapMetro says the airport bus stop is on the lower level just outside baggage claim. Route 20 Manor Road and Riverside starts there, every 15 to 30 minutes, seven days a week, and runs through Riverside before downtown and the University of Texas. The agency calls that a 35 minute ride to downtown. A single ride is $1.25. The page says a tap day is capped at two rides, which it describes as $2.50, and that rail uses a commuter fare capped at $7 a service day. Route 483 Night Owl runs after midnight to 4 a.m. and ends downtown on 6th Street. Luggage on a city bus is a real constraint. A dawn departure that misses the last useful bus is a rideshare or a hotel shuttle, not a hope.

The airport ground transportation page says rideshare and taxi pickup for people who just landed is under the consolidated rental car facility, just north or behind the Red Garage. Rideshare and taxi drop off for people flying out uses the upper level. The same page says both the upper and lower roadways are open for ordinary passenger drop off, and it names Route 20 as the bus. We do not publish a live rideshare fare or a live traffic time. Pair the flight itself with flights to Austin when you are still choosing the arrival day.

Hilton Austin Airport

Hilton Austin Airport, 9515 Hotel Drive, Austin, Texas 78719, is the on airport answer in this set. The location page says it is the only full service hotel on the grounds. The shuttle is complimentary and 24 hours, and the page limits it to the main terminal. Austin Bergstrom is listed at 0 miles. The city center is listed at 7 miles. Self parking is $15 a day, on site, with in and out privileges. Valet is not available. Electric vehicle charging is listed as nearby, at 1 mile, not in the garage. The page does not print a check in time, a room count, or a pool note. We are not filling those blanks. Call 1 800 445 8667, which is the number that page prints, or open the hotel site and read the room you would actually book.

Holiday Inn Austin Airport

Holiday Inn Austin Airport, 6711 East Ben White Boulevard, Austin, Texas 78741, says it is two miles from the airport and that guests get a complimentary 24 hour shuttle and free parking. The FAQ on the same site then says self parking is $15 a day, $15.00 a night for registered guests, with in and out privileges. We are not choosing free over $15. Ask which line is on your confirmation. The FAQ also says pets are not allowed. The page title still says the hotel is pet friendly. Believe the FAQ unless the hotel tells you otherwise in writing. Check in is 3:00 p.m. Check out is 12:00 p.m. The minimum age is 21. The front desk number is 512 385 8400. The hotel describes a resort style pool, a fitness center it calls 24 hour, on site dining at G Bistro, and a bar. The pool FAQ says there is one outdoor pool, open seasonally, and that it is not heated. The shuttle FAQ says the pickup is the upper level near Delta, in the hotel shuttle pickup area. That sentence is the hotel's. Confirm the door when you land, because airline counters move.

Courtyard by Marriott Austin Airport

Courtyard by Marriott Austin Airport, 7809 East Ben White Boulevard, Austin, Texas 78741, lists the airport at 2.0 miles and says the hotel is 7 minutes away. Phone 512 386 7464. The complimentary shuttle runs from 4:00 a.m. to midnight. You call the desk to request a pickup from the airport. Shuttles toward the airport run every 30 minutes, and the page says to reserve a seat at check in. A departure before 4:00 a.m. is outside that window. Check in is 3:00 p.m. Check out is 12:00 p.m. The minimum age is 18. Self parking is $17 a day. Pets are welcome, up to two, 25 pounds each, and they cannot be left unattended. The non refundable pet fee is $100 a stay. The hotel lists electric vehicle charging. The Bistro is the on site restaurant. The FAQ says the hotel does not offer free breakfast. The introduction says free Wi Fi. The amenity list says complimentary Wi Fi for Marriott Bonvoy members who book direct. Read which one your rate includes. This is a useful flight hotel inside its shuttle hours. It is not a lake hotel.

Staybridge Suites Austin Airport

Staybridge Suites Austin Airport, 1611 Airport Commerce Drive, Austin, Texas 78741, is the suite hotel for a longer stay, not a terminal walk. Check in is 3:00 p.m. Check out is 11:00 a.m. The minimum age is 21. The front desk is 512 389 9767. The directions page offers complimentary parking and says the airport shuttle is not available. There is also no local shuttle. The FAQ says dogs only, up to 50 pounds each, maximum of two, with a non refundable pet fee ranging from $75 to $150, and a pet agreement signed at check in. The fee sentence on the page is compressed, so ask which dollar matches your dates. The hotel says it has an outdoor pool, an on site fitness center, and free Wi Fi. A highlight says the hotel is less than 10 miles from Circuit of the Americas. The driving note from the airport sends you west on Highway 71, then right on Riverside Drive and right on Airport Commerce Drive. It does not print miles or minutes. If you do not have a car, budget a rideshare from the Red Garage pickup. Do not expect a van.

What this page will not pretend

We do not publish a live drive time from the Hilton's 7 city center miles, the Holiday Inn's two miles, or the Courtyard's 2.0 miles and 7 minutes. Those numbers measure different doors, and one of them is a marketing minute. Festival weekends, football nights, and race weekends at Circuit of the Americas change the same road. CapMetro's 35 minute bus ride is the agency's own description of Route 20, not a promise at 5 p.m. on a Friday. We do not publish nightly room rates. We do not rank these four against South Congress hotels, because they are a different product. For that product, use best luxury hotels in Austin.

What to know before you book

What is the best hotel near Austin Airport for a flight night?

For a night that exists to catch the flight, open Hilton Austin Airport first. Its location page says it is the only full service hotel on Austin Bergstrom International Airport grounds, with a complimentary shuttle 24 hours to the main terminal, and it lists the airport at 0 miles and the city center at 7 miles. Holiday Inn Austin Airport, at 6711 East Ben White Boulevard, says it is two miles from the airport and prints a complimentary 24 hour shuttle. Courtyard by Marriott Austin Airport, at 7809 East Ben White Boulevard, lists the airport at 2.0 miles and a complimentary shuttle from 4:00 a.m. to midnight. Staybridge Suites Austin Airport does not offer an airport shuttle. None of these four is a South Congress or Lady Bird Lake luxury hotel. Use the Austin hotel guide for that night.

Should I stay at the airport, nearby, or in the city?

Stay on the airport grounds when the flight is the whole point and you want the Hilton shuttle the location page says runs 24 hours. Stay nearby on Ben White Boulevard when you want a pool or a restaurant and you have read the shuttle window, especially the Courtyard gap after midnight. Stay in South Congress, on Lady Bird Lake, on Rainey Street, or downtown when the trip is the city. There is no train from the terminal into downtown. CapMetro says Route 20 is a 35 minute ride to downtown. A downtown hotel's own note is not a promise for these airport doors. The Austin hotel guide records Austin Proper's description of the drive as about 15 to 20 minutes, and that hotel says it does not run a shuttle.

How do I get from AUS to downtown without a car?

CapMetro says the airport stop for Route 20 is on the lower level just outside baggage claim. Route 20 runs every 15 to 30 minutes, seven days a week, through Riverside before downtown and the University of Texas, and the agency calls it a 35 minute ride to downtown. The single ride is $1.25, and the bus day cap the page describes is $2.50. Route 483 runs after midnight to 4 a.m. The airport ground transportation page says rideshare and taxi pickup for arriving passengers is under the consolidated rental car facility, just north or behind the Red Garage, and that rideshare and taxi drop off for departing passengers uses the upper level. There is no rail line from the terminal into downtown.

Which airport hotels run a shuttle, and when does it stop?

Hilton Austin Airport says the complimentary shuttle runs 24 hours to the main terminal only. Holiday Inn Austin Airport says the complimentary shuttle is 24 hours, and the FAQ prints the pickup as the upper level near Delta in the hotel shuttle pickup area. Courtyard by Marriott Austin Airport says the complimentary shuttle runs from 4:00 a.m. to midnight, that you should call 512 386 7464 to request a pickup, and that shuttles to the airport run every 30 minutes. Staybridge Suites Austin Airport says an airport shuttle is not available. A flight outside a printed window needs a different hotel or a rideshare. Confirm the next run with the desk before you rely on it.
