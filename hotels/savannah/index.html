Hotels in Savannah, Georgia

Savannah’s Historic District squares reward walkable stays; Midtown and islands trade price for distance. Qualitative guidance for US travelers — compare rates on partner sites when you are ready.

Editorial guidance only. We do not sell lodging, collect payment, or act as merchant of record. Prices and availability change — verify on the partner site before you book. See our Disclaimer and Affiliate Disclosure.

Neighborhoods at a glance

Historic District

Squares, River Street, and dining within walking distance. Expect carriage traffic and weekend crowds.

South Historic / Victorian District edges

Often a calmer night while remaining close to the core.

Midtown Savannah

Practical pricing; short drive or scooter to the squares.

Tybee Island

Beach focus — pair with day trips into Savannah rather than as a pure historic base.

Practical tips

  • River Street nightlife can be loud — ask about courtyard or upper-floor rooms when comparing partners.
  • St. Patrick’s Day and major festivals spike rates; flexible dates help.
