Best luxury & boutique hotels in Savannah, Georgia

Savannah’s high-ticket lodging map splits between Historic District squares and courtyard boutiques, riverfront energy, and calmer South Historic edges — choose the neighborhood before you fall for a suite photo. Qualitative guidance for travelers planning a high-end Hostess City trip — we name specific editorial picks and link to official hotel websites.

Best luxury neighborhoods for a splurge

Historic District boutique inns

Squares, dining, and courtyard suites within walking distance of Forsyth Park and the river. Upper floors and interior courtyards often sleep better on festival weekends.

South Historic / Victorian edges

Calmer nights while remaining close to the core — strong when you want porch energy without River Street volume.

Riverfront / Eastern Wharf premium

Views and modern lifestyle hotels where the Historic District meets the waterfront; verify noise notes for sleep-focused anniversaries.

Midtown polished hotels

When Historic District boutique inventory is sold out — treat as a short-ride base, not a square-front substitute.

Tybee Island resort days

Beach-first luxury paired with day trips into Savannah — weaker as a pure Historic District walking base.

The Perry Lane Hotel

Historic District

Luxury Collection flagship on East Perry Street with residential-feel rooms, a strong art program, and walkable square access — a clear anniversary or high-ticket first Savannah stay.

Official website

Thompson Savannah

Eastern Wharf / riverfront

Contemporary lifestyle luxury where the Historic District meets the Savannah River — ideal when you want modern design energy and waterfront access without giving up downtown day trips.

Official website

The Drayton Hotel

Historic District / City Hall corridor

Intimate Curio Collection boutique in a 19th-century building with rooftop terrace views and Lowcountry dining — strong when you want a compact, design-led downtown base.

Official website

Andaz Savannah

Ellis Square / Historic District

Design-forward Andaz on Ellis Square steps from City Market and the river — a polished urban boutique when shopping, dining, and walkability set the itinerary.

Official website

Kimpton Brice Hotel

Washington Square / near River Street

Practical tips for a premium stay

  • St. Patrick’s Day, major festivals, and peak spring weekends spike Historic District luxury rates — compare early.
  • Ask about courtyard or upper-floor categories near River Street nightlife if sleep is the priority.
  • Parking and destination fees vary by property — compare totals on the official hotel site.
