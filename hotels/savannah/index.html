Best luxury & boutique hotels in Savannah, Georgia

Savannah’s Historic District squares reward walkable boutique inns and design hotels — treat Midtown and island stays as intentional trade-offs on a high-ticket trip. Qualitative guidance for travelers planning a high-end trip — compare rates on partner sites when you are ready.

Best luxury neighborhoods for a splurge
