Best luxury neighborhoods for a splurge

Historic District boutique inns

Squares, dining, and courtyard suites within walking distance of Forsyth Park and the river. Upper floors and interior courtyards often sleep better on festival weekends. The default leisure luxury base when walking is the point.

South Historic / Victorian edges

Calmer nights while remaining close to the core. Strong when you want porch energy without River Street volume.

Riverfront / Eastern Wharf premium

Views and modern lifestyle hotels where the Historic District meets the waterfront. Verify noise notes for sleep-focused trips. Useful when river light and newer public spaces matter as much as a square-front door.

Midtown polished hotels

When Historic District boutique inventory is sold out. Treat as a short-ride base, not a square-front substitute.

Tybee Island resort days

Beach-first luxury paired with day trips into Savannah. Weaker as a pure Historic District walking base. Keep the hotel in town if the squares are the trip.

What to know before you book

How loud is River Street compared with a Historic District courtyard?

River Street and the Bay Street edge can run loud on weekend and festival nights. Ask for a courtyard, an interior room, or an upper floor if sleep is the priority. Squares a few blocks inland often sleep calmer after dinner. We do not invent noise scores. Ask the hotel when you book.

Can I walk the Historic District from these hotels?

Yes for the Historic District and Ellis Square picks. Perry Lane, The Drayton, Andaz Savannah, and Kimpton Brice sit in walking range of squares, Forsyth Park approaches, and downtown dining. Thompson Savannah is on Eastern Wharf, so plan a short ride or a longer waterfront walk into the core squares.

Courtyard inn or riverfront hotel?

Choose a Historic District courtyard or square-adjacent boutique when walking and quieter nights come first. Choose riverfront or Eastern Wharf when views and modern lifestyle space matter more. Confirm room location on the official site. Street-facing river rooms can pick up nightlife.

How should I treat St. Patrick’s Day or other festival weekends?

St. Patrick’s Day, spring garden weeks, and major festivals rewrite Historic District availability. Book earlier on the official site. Read cancellation terms before you pay. Flexible terms matter more than a prepaid discount if dates might shift.

Should I stay on Tybee Island or use it as a day trip?

Use Tybee as a beach day from a Historic District or riverfront base if the squares are the point of the trip. A Tybee resort is a different product. It is weaker as a walking base for Forsyth Park and the river core.
