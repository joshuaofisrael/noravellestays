Best luxury & boutique hotels in Columbus, Ohio

Columbus luxury starts with a neighborhood, not a skyline photo. The Short North puts galleries, High Street dinners, and Gallery Hop at the door, and it asks you to decide whether you want the art hotel at 620 North High Street or the collegiate hotel a few blocks north. Downtown puts an Art Deco tower on Broad Street and the Scioto Mile. The Arena District puts Nationwide Arena, North Market, and the convention center within a short walk, and it asks you to protect sleep from the event. Franklinton, west of the river, puts COSI and a 198 room independent hotel on Belle Street, which is not a High Street stroll. German Village, the Brewery District, Victorian Village, and Grandview Heights are real evenings and mornings. None of the hotels in this set use those addresses. The Ohio State campus has one on-campus full service hotel. Easton Town Center has a Hilton inside the shops, a ride from downtown. John Glenn Columbus International Airport has no train into the city. Choose the neighborhood first. Then open the official hotel site for the pick that fits.

Best luxury neighborhoods for a splurge

Short North Arts District

The Short North is the walkable luxury core when the trip is galleries, a long dinner, and one intentional night on High Street. It is a weak place to assume Ohio Stadium is next door, and it is a weak place to assume German Village brick streets are the same block. The Short North Alliance says Gallery Hop has run on the first Saturday of every month since 1984, from 4 p.m. to 8 p.m., and that the evening is free. The alliance office is at 875 North High Street. Galleries, sidewalk performances, and restaurant specials stack on that Saturday. An ordinary Tuesday is a different street. Ask for a room off High Street if you came for the art and not for the sidewalk. We do not invent a noise score. Confirm the next Saturday on the alliance page, because a public calendar can still move.

Le Méridien Columbus, The Joseph, at 620 North High Street, is the art hotel in this set. The hotel's wedding site describes 135 guestrooms and suites. The developer's project page for the same address also describes 135 keys, a fitness center, two spa suites, and direct access to the signature restaurant. The hotel's own journey page describes The Guild House for dining, Soul for cocktails, Liliana Spa Suites for in-house treatments, and more than 500 pieces from the Pizzuti Collection, with a gallery display on the second floor. Marriott's rooms page describes a renovation with Nespresso machines in the rooms. One hundred thirty five rooms means a Gallery Hop Saturday or a wedding weekend can sell the useful suite while a much larger downtown hotel still shows inventory. The wedding FAQ says there is no hotel shuttle. It lists overnight valet at $50 with unlimited in and out, or $55 for an oversized vehicle, in the Joseph Garage on Russell Street. Those figures are the wedding FAQ's, so read the total on the page you book. A High Street view is the reason many people choose the hotel. It is also the reason to ask for a quieter room if sleep matters more than the mural across the street.

Graduate by Hilton Columbus is the other High Street pick, at 750 North High Street, still in the Short North and closer to campus than The Joseph. The old address graduatehotels.com/columbus/ redirects to that Hilton page. The hotel is open under the Graduate name. It is not a closure and it is not a new building. A Hilton travel article places it in the Short North, with Homage Bar for breakfast, coffee, and cocktails, a Shake Shack in the building, free Wi-Fi, a fitness center, pet-friendly rooms, Digital Key, and valet parking. The same company's game-day article says rooms spread across 11 floors and describes an 8 minute drive to Ohio Stadium. Treat that drive as the article's planning note. Game-day traffic is not an 8 minute promise. The article does not publish a valet rate or a pet fee, so we do not invent either. Ask for a room off High Street on a football Saturday. Seven hundred fifty North High is a Short North pillow with collegiate design. It is not the on-campus hotel. Use The Blackwell when you need to be on campus. Use Graduate when you want High Street and a straightforward ride to the stadium.

Victorian Village

Victorian Village is the residential neighborhood west of High Street, with Goodale Park as the green between those houses and the Short North shops. None of the Noravelle picks use a Victorian Village side street. That is a gap in luxury inventory, not a hotel we forgot. Sleep at The Joseph or Graduate and walk to the park. Sleep at Canopy, whose 2019 opening release named the rooftop Goodale Station and said the roof looks toward Goodale Park, downtown, and the Short North. The park is an outing and a view. It is not a separate hotel cluster. A room on a quiet Victorian Village street would be a different product from a High Street hotel, and this list does not have one. If the trip is mostly the park and a quiet house, say so when you choose between The Joseph, Graduate, and Canopy, and still ask which side of the building faces the street. Do not assume a gallery Saturday and a side-street silence are the same walk.

Downtown and the LeVeque Tower

Hotel LeVeque, Autograph Collection, at 50 West Broad Street, is the historic tower hotel in this set. The hotel's own site describes 149 guest rooms, including six suites, with Scioto River Mile views, and The Keep as the modern French brasserie and bar. The site's own name for the hotel is Hotel LeVeque, Autograph Collection, and the page links to the Marriott overview for the property. Broad Street here is downtown, south of the Arena District and north of German Village. You can walk toward the riverfront and ride or walk north toward the convention center. You should not pretend 50 West Broad is the middle of High Street in the Short North. The offers page describes a Park and Stay package that includes valet for one vehicle per night and calls that valet a $50 value. That is the hotel's package language, not a rate sheet we are willing to freeze as the only price. Read the current total before you compare it with a Short North garage. One hundred forty nine rooms is a boutique tower. It can sell out on a convention week while Hilton Columbus Downtown still shows rooms. Ask for a higher floor if the Broad Street traffic is not the bedroom you wanted. The Keep is the reason many people stay in the building. It is also a restaurant the neighborhood uses, so a lively dining room is part of the product.

Arena District and the convention center

The Arena District is the downtown entertainment map beside the Greater Columbus Convention Center. Nationwide Arena, North Market, and the convention floor can all set the night. It is a strong base when the game, the show, or the meeting is the trip. It is a weak place to assume Short North quiet. Hilton Columbus Downtown, at 402 North High Street, is the large hotel in this set. The hotel's overview says it is the largest hotel in Ohio, with 1,000 guest rooms and 75,000 square feet of meeting space, and that a second tower was added in fall 2022. It describes FYR Short North, Spark, and Stories on High on the 28th floor, an indoor pool, two fitness centers, and a local art collection of nearly 400 pieces. A fact sheet on the hotel site says the hotel connects to the convention center by a skybridge. The overview places Nationwide Arena and North Market within walking distance, along with more than 150 dining and entertainment venues. Check-in is 3 p.m. at 402 North High Street. Check-out is noon. The overview lists self parking at $43 a day and valet at $50 a day, both with in and out privileges. Valet for FYR, Spark, or Stories on High is listed at $10 with validation. The same page lists a nonrefundable pet fee of $50, with a stated maximum weight of 125 pounds, a $75 per night incidental hold, and a 1 percent Short North community reinvestment charge on the guest room and on restaurant charges. It describes John Glenn Columbus International Airport as 8 miles away and Rickenbacker as 17 miles away. One thousand rooms absorb a citywide meeting that would empty The Joseph. The skybridge works both ways: the convention is convenient, and a ballroom night can be the soundtrack. Ask for a higher, quieter floor on arena and convention weeks. Huntington Park and Lower.com Field sit in the same district. A Clippers game or a Crew match is another reason to make that request. We do not publish a team schedule.

Canopy by Hilton Columbus Downtown Short North, at 77 East Nationwide Boulevard, is the lifestyle hotel between that arena map and the Short North. Hilton's July 30, 2019 opening release described 167 rooms, including four suites, Central Market House on the ground floor, and Goodale Station as the rooftop. It placed the hotel at the nexus of the Short North and the Arena District. The release also described a complimentary evening tasting for guests. That line is seven years old. Confirm on the live Hilton page whether the tasting, the rooftop hours, and the parking fee still match the opening announcement before you treat them as included. We do not publish a current Canopy valet rate because the opening release did not give one we are willing to freeze. Seventy seven East Nationwide is close enough to walk to the convention center, North Market, and the south end of the Short North, and far enough from 620 North High that a rainy night is a walk you notice. Ask which rooms face the busier side of Nationwide if the arena is the outing and not the bedroom. This is not a 1,000 room convention hotel, and it is not The Joseph. That is why it is on the list.

Franklinton and the Scioto Peninsula

The Junto, at 77 Belle Street, is the independent hotel west of the Scioto River. The hotel's FAQ says it has 198 guest rooms, including 31 suites. Check-in is after 4 p.m. Check-out is at noon. The minimum check-in age is 21. A cancellation must be made by 4 p.m. 48 hours before arrival to avoid a fee of one night's room and tax. The FAQ lists a destination amenity fee of $20 per night, which it says includes the Trade Room and the Gear Garage. The Gear Garage, the FAQ says, stocks e-bikes, cruiser bikes, longboards, and cameras for guests. Overnight valet is $50 per vehicle with in and out privileges, at the front entrance on Belle Street. The garage behind the hotel has electric vehicle chargers, and the FAQ says valet guests can ask to have a car charged based on availability. The hotel says John Glenn airport is 10 miles away. It names Little West Tavern, Maudine's, and Brass Eye in the building, and it says the hotel sits inside a designated outdoor refreshment area. It describes COSI as steps from the front doors, and Franklinton Fridays as a monthly gallery evening. Franklinton is a creative district and a family museum night. It is not the Short North, and the river is the reason. A downtown dinner is a bridge or a ride, not a stroll off High Street. One hundred ninety eight rooms is still a house that can sell out when a convention and a campus weekend share a calendar. Ask for a quieter room if the lawn and the tavern are the reason you came and the bedroom is not.

German Village

German Village is the brick-street neighborhood south of downtown, with Schiller Park, the Book Loft, and a dinner map that people plan as its own evening. None of the city picks in this set use a German Village address. That is a gap in staffed luxury hotels, not a hidden inn we left off the page. Sleep at Hotel LeVeque if you want the tower and a short ride south. Sleep in the Short North if the galleries are the day and the village is one dinner. Do not book a downtown suite and expect to stroll home through Schiller Park after midnight without a ride. Furnished loft rentals operate in the neighborhood. They are a different product from Hotel LeVeque or The Joseph, and they are not Noravelle picks. The old idea of the Worthington Inn as a suburban hotel night is also the wrong map. See the names section below. German Village is the outing. The pillow is downtown, in the Short North, or, if the shops are the whole trip, at Easton.

Brewery District

The Brewery District sits south of downtown along Front Street, between the capitol grid and German Village. Breweries, patios, and a dinner are the product. None of these picks sleep there. Hotel LeVeque is the closer tower. Hilton Columbus Downtown and Canopy make it a longer ride at the end of the night. The Short North makes it a planned outing rather than a walk before coffee. Use the district when one evening is the point. Do not use it as a substitute address for a luxury hotel that is not on this list. If every smaller room downtown is gone for a festival weekend, the 1,000 room Hilton is the inventory, not a reason to invent a boutique on Front Street.

University District and the Ohio State campus

The Blackwell Inn, at 2110 Tuttle Park Place, is the campus hotel. The hotel says it is Ohio State's only on-campus hotel, with 151 guestrooms and suites. On-site dining is 2110 and Simon's lounge. The contact page lists valet at $35 per evening with in and out privileges, not included in the nightly rate unless a package says so. Day use valet is $20. Special event valet starts at $50. Self parking is in the Lane Avenue garage next door. The hotel says it does not operate that garage, that you pay each time you exit, and that there are no in and out privileges. It also says the garage operator can charge more for special events with little notice. The front doors stay locked from midnight to 6 a.m. Call ahead if you will arrive later. The hotel offers a complimentary car service for guests, based on availability, inside a three mile radius. It names Grandview Heights, the Short North, the other side of campus, and the Ohio State Wexner Medical Center as trips that radius can cover. It says the service does not run in and out of stadium or arena events because of the traffic, and that a car outside the radius can be arranged for a fee. This is the right base for commencement, a campus conference, a recruiting weekend, or a game when you want to be on campus before the traffic. It is the wrong base if you wanted Gallery Hop at the door and did not ask. One hundred fifty one rooms can disappear for graduation while the downtown Hilton still shows inventory. From the airport, the hotel's own directions use I-670, I-71, and SR-315 to Lane Avenue. That is a driving note, not a train.

Grandview Heights

Grandview Heights is the dining neighborhood west of campus, along Grandview Avenue. It is an evening, not a hotel cluster in this set. None of the Noravelle picks use a Grandview address. The Blackwell is the hotel that names it, because the complimentary car service described above can cover it when a car is free. A Short North hotel makes Grandview a rideshare. A downtown hotel makes it a longer ride at the end of the night. An Easton hotel makes it a cross-town plan you should not stack onto an arrival evening. We do not publish a live drive time from Tuttle Park Place to Grandview Avenue. If the trip is mostly Grandview patios, say so when you choose The Blackwell, and still plan the return. The car service is availability, not a private chauffeur you can assume at midnight after a game. The hotel already told you the stadium is outside that promise.

Easton

Hilton Columbus at Easton, at 3900 Chagrin Drive, is the full service hotel inside Easton Town Center. The hotel's page says it is connected to the shopping complex, with an indoor pool and a fitness center. It lists check-in at 4 p.m. and check-out at 11 a.m. It describes John Glenn airport as four miles away, and it says downtown, the Franklin Park Conservatory, Ohio State, and German Village are within 20 minutes. Those times are the hotel's planning notes, not a promise in traffic. Easton is the right splurge when the shops and the restaurants in the complex are the trip, or when a dawn flight matters more than a High Street midnight. It is the wrong splurge if you wanted to walk to Gallery Hop. COTA lists an Easton Transit Center at 4260 Stelzer Road, with a ticket machine, so a bus day is possible. It is still a plan, not a sidewalk. We do not publish an Easton valet rate because the page we read did not give a figure we are willing to freeze. Read the total on the hotel site. Polaris, farther north, is a commercial strip with a different hotel set. It is not where these picks sleep.

Near CMH

Full service hotels beside John Glenn Columbus International Airport suit a dawn departure or a late landing. They are logistics for the flight, separate from a Short North, downtown, Franklinton, campus, or Easton splurge. None of the Noravelle picks sleep in an airport corridor. The airport's ground transportation page says rideshare pickup, including Uber and Lyft, is on the arrivals level. After you reserve the ride, go to level 1, cross the arrivals drive to the ground transportation area, and stand between the columns to the right. Taxis are on the ground transportation level. The page points to COTA's trip planner. It does not describe a train into downtown, and it does not tell you to look for a retired airport shuttle brand. Hilton Columbus at Easton is the closest pick in this set, at the four miles the hotel describes, which is still a ride. Hilton Columbus Downtown describes 8 miles. The Junto describes 10 miles. Those numbers do not match because they measure different doors. None of them is a live traffic time. We do not publish a taxi fare. Use an airport hotel only when the flight sets the night, then move to the neighborhood that fits the rest of the trip.

How to choose

Pick the job of the trip before you compare suite photos. A gallery weekend wants The Joseph, or Graduate if you also want a collegiate hotel closer to campus. A historic downtown tower wants Hotel LeVeque. A convention, an arena night, or a North Market morning with more than one room type left wants Hilton Columbus Downtown. A lifestyle hotel between the arena and the Short North wants Canopy. A Franklinton night, with COSI and a river between you and High Street, wants The Junto. A campus conference, a commencement, or a game-day base on campus wants The Blackwell. A shopping weekend at Easton wants Hilton Columbus at Easton, and it wants a ride if the rest of the city is also on the list. German Village, the Brewery District, Victorian Village, and Grandview Heights are outings. They are not missing hotels. A stadium day wants a ride from whichever downtown or Short North hotel you already chose, or a campus hotel that has already told you it will not drive into the stadium traffic.

Smaller hotels sell out first. The Joseph's wedding site describes 135 guestrooms and suites. Hotel LeVeque describes 149, including six suites. The Blackwell describes 151. Hilton's opening release for Canopy described 167, including four suites. The Junto's FAQ says 198, including 31 suites. Hilton Columbus Downtown describes 1,000. A larger hotel can still show rooms when a High Street suite is gone. The table is an editorial fit guide, not a ranking and not a price list. We do not invent nightly rates. Open the official site named in the last column before you pay. Read how we pick luxury hotels if you want the rules behind that short list, and how pricing works for why the compare buttons on this page stay closed.

Timing, seasonality, and event weekends

Ohio State home football is the inventory shock with a map attached. Ohio Stadium is on campus. Every Short North and downtown hotel in this set is a ride away. The Blackwell is the on-campus hotel, and it has already said its complimentary car will not run into stadium traffic. Graduate's Hilton article describes an 8 minute drive. Use that only as a calm-day note. Commencement and recruiting weekends fill the same campus hotels and then spill onto High Street. Book the room type you actually want, and read the cancellation window. We do not invent a season schedule or a nightly rate. A 135 room Short North hotel can disappear while the 1,000 room Hilton still shows rooms. That is a reason to decide the neighborhood early, not a reason to take a prepaid rate you cannot cancel if the game moves.

The convention center is the downtown version of the same problem. Hilton Columbus Downtown is attached by the skybridge the hotel's fact sheet describes, so a citywide show in the building is not a rumor. Ask. Nationwide Arena nights stack the same blocks. North Market mornings are the calmer use of that address. Gallery Hop is monthly and smaller. It changes High Street from 4 p.m. to 8 p.m. on the first Saturday. It does not rewrite the whole city the way a stadium Saturday does. Confirm the date on the Short North Alliance page and the wider calendar on Experience Columbus.

Summers are hot and humid. An indoor pool at Hilton Columbus Downtown, at the Blackwell if you are on campus, or at Hilton Columbus at Easton is a practical amenity in July and August, not a postcard extra. The Joseph's journey page describes Liliana Spa Suites as bookable treatments, not as a pool. The Junto describes bikes in the Gear Garage, which are more useful in mild weather than in a heat advisory. Plan shade, water, and a lighter first afternoon between the Short North and German Village. If heat already limits your day, ask your own clinician before you stack a long outdoor walk. Winters are a cold walk. Ice changes the case for a car between Easton, Grandview, and dinner. We do not publish a forecast. A prepaid discount is the wrong tool when the dates are tied to a game or a graduation you might not attend.

Noise, arenas, and High Street

The Short North is lively because High Street is the product. Gallery Hop, open doors, and game-day crowds stack on the sidewalk in front of The Joseph and Graduate. Arena nights are the louder downtown version, at Nationwide Arena and along the convention blocks. We do not invent decibel scores. If you want that energy for one planned evening, stay at Hilton Columbus Downtown or Canopy and ask for a higher floor. If you want the show as an outing and not as a bedroom, sleep in the Short North or at Hotel LeVeque, and rideshare back. The Junto is the in-between on a different map: west of the river, with its own lawn and tavern, and still a social hotel. The Blackwell is the campus version. Locked doors from midnight to 6 a.m. tell you the hotel expects a quieter night than High Street, and a campus event can still fill the lobby.

The Joseph has a gallery floor and a High Street address. Both are the appeal. Graduate has Homage Bar and a sidewalk. Hotel LeVeque has The Keep. Canopy's opening release described a rooftop. The Junto has a destination fee that is paying for a social ground floor, so read what the $20 includes and ask for a room off the tavern if you wanted the bikes and not the bar. Hilton Columbus Downtown has the convention skybridge, Stories on High, and an indoor pool. The Blackwell has meeting floors and a campus address. Hilton Columbus at Easton has a shopping complex at the door. Tell the hotel which side you want, and keep that note with the reservation. A phone request that never lands in the booking record is not a plan.

Getting there, parking, and the bus

Parking is a hotel-by-hotel number, not a citywide one. The Junto's FAQ lists overnight valet at $50 with in and out, and a $20 destination amenity fee. The Blackwell lists valet at $35 per evening with in and out, day use at $20, and event valet from $50, and it says the Lane Avenue garage is not the hotel's. Hilton Columbus Downtown's overview lists self parking at $43 and valet at $50, both with in and out, plus the 1 percent community charge and a $50 pet fee. The Joseph's wedding FAQ lists overnight valet at $50, or $55 oversized, in the garage on Russell Street. Hotel LeVeque's offers page calls included valet a $50 value inside a package. A second car, a cabana, a spa treatment, and a late checkout can all sit outside the headline rate. We do not publish a valet price for Canopy, Graduate, or Hilton Columbus at Easton, because those pages did not give a figure we are willing to freeze here. Read the total on each official site. A car is useful for airport luggage, Easton, Grandview, German Village, and the stadium. It is a burden downtown on a concert weekend, when garages fill and the scenic block is not a place to circle.

COTA's fare page lists a $2 two hour pass for standard, frequent, and rush hour service, and a $4.50 day pass. With the Transit app or a registered Smartcard, the page says you never pay more than $4.50 a day or $62 a calendar month. Children age 4 and under ride at no cost. Reduced fares are $1 for a two hour pass for riders who qualify, including people 65 and older. The page says service changes come in January, May, and September. There is a ticket machine at John Glenn arrivals and a Customer Experience Center at 33 North High Street. There is no streetcar, and there is no downtown rail from the airport. For a city that does have a free streetcar between a plaza and an arena, compare Kansas City luxury hotels. For a mountain city with an airport train into a station hotel, compare Denver luxury hotels. For a downtown music strip rather than a gallery hop, compare Nashville luxury hotels. Columbus can still work without a car if the sleep is on High Street, Broad Street, or in the Arena District and the outing is one neighborhood. Add a car when the plan needs Easton, Grandview, the stadium, or a late German Village dinner. From CMH, use the arrivals-level rideshare instructions above, then go to the neighborhood that matches the arrival hour.

How to shape the weekend

A clean luxury weekend has one sleep neighborhood and one intentional outing off that map. Land, check the official room location, and do not schedule the Short North, German Village, the Arena District, and a campus game on the same arrival evening. If you sleep at The Joseph or Graduate, walk to dinner nearby and pick one window for Goodale Park, the North Market, or a German Village ride. If you sleep at Hilton Columbus Downtown or Canopy, treat the Short North as a bus or rideshare outing and ask for a higher floor before the arena. If you sleep at Hotel LeVeque, give the river and The Keep the first night, and ride south only if German Village is the one outing. If you sleep at The Junto, keep COSI and Franklinton as the neighborhood and downtown as a bridge. If you sleep at The Blackwell, give campus the day, and use the three mile car only when the hotel confirms it. If you sleep at Hilton Columbus at Easton, let the complex be the day. There is no separate Columbus where-to-stay guide or day plan on this site yet. This page is that guide. If the dates are still loose, read how pricing works before you treat a prepaid game-day rate as the careful choice. Questions about a listing can go through contact.

What to know before you book

Which neighborhood fits a Columbus luxury stay?

Short North hotels, including Le Méridien Columbus, The Joseph at 620 North High Street and Graduate by Hilton Columbus at 750 North High Street, suit galleries, High Street dinners, and Gallery Hop. Victorian Village is the residential side west of High Street, with Goodale Park, and none of these picks sleep on a Victorian Village side street. Hotel LeVeque, Autograph Collection, at 50 West Broad Street suits a historic downtown tower and the Scioto Mile. Hilton Columbus Downtown at 402 North High Street suits the convention center, Nationwide Arena, and North Market. Canopy by Hilton Columbus Downtown Short North at 77 East Nationwide Boulevard sits at the edge of the Short North and the Arena District. The Junto at 77 Belle Street suits Franklinton, on the west side of the Scioto River, with COSI nearby. German Village and the Brewery District are real evenings south of downtown, and none of these picks sleep there. The Blackwell Inn at 2110 Tuttle Park Place suits the Ohio State campus. Grandview Heights is a dining outing the Blackwell says its local car service can reach, not a hotel in this set. Hilton Columbus at Easton at 3900 Chagrin Drive suits Easton Town Center. Airport hotels beside John Glenn Columbus International Airport are logistics. Choose the neighborhood before you compare suite photos.

Should I choose the Short North, downtown, the Arena District, or the University District?

Choose the Short North when galleries, High Street, and a smaller art hotel are the point. Choose Hotel LeVeque when you want the Art Deco tower on Broad Street and you can walk or ride to the Arena District. Choose Hilton Columbus Downtown or Canopy when the convention center, Nationwide Arena, or North Market is the center of gravity, and ask for a higher floor on an event night. Choose The Blackwell when the campus, a commencement, or a game day base matters more than a Short North midnight. German Village, the Brewery District, Victorian Village, and Grandview Heights are outings from those bases. Easton is a different map, at the shopping complex, not a walk from High Street. Confirm fees and cancellation on each official site.

How loud is the Short North compared with the Arena District?

High Street can run lively on weekend nights, Gallery Hop, and Ohio State game days. Gallery Hop is the first Saturday of the month, 4 p.m. to 8 p.m., and the Short North Alliance says it is free. The Joseph and Graduate both sit on High Street, so ask for a room off the street if sleep matters more than the sidewalk. The Arena District, Nationwide Arena, and the blocks beside the convention center carry a different sound on concert nights, Blue Jackets games, and citywide meetings. Hilton Columbus Downtown is connected to the convention center. Canopy sits on East Nationwide Boulevard at the edge of that district and the Short North. The Junto is west of the river in Franklinton. The hotel describes Franklinton Fridays and a designated outdoor refreshment area, so the block can be social, and it is not High Street. The Blackwell follows a campus rhythm. The hotel says its front doors stay locked from midnight to 6 a.m. German Village brick streets are usually the calmer outing. We do not invent noise scores. Ask the hotel when you book.

What fees should I expect, and how should cancellation work?

Valet, self parking, destination or community fees, pet fees, and taxes can change the total. The Junto's FAQ lists a destination amenity fee of $20 per night and overnight valet at $50 per vehicle with in and out privileges. It says check-in is after 4 p.m., check-out is at noon, the minimum check-in age is 21, and a cancellation must be made by 4 p.m. 48 hours before arrival to avoid a fee of one night's room and tax. The Blackwell lists valet at $35 per evening with in and out privileges, day use valet at $20, and special event valet starting at $50. Self parking is in the Lane Avenue garage next door, which the hotel says it does not operate, with no in and out privileges. Hilton Columbus Downtown's own overview lists self parking at $43 a day and valet at $50 a day, both with in and out privileges, a nonrefundable pet fee of $50, check-in at 3 p.m., check-out at noon, and a $75 per night incidental hold. The same page describes a 1 percent Short North community reinvestment charge on the room and on restaurant charges. The Joseph's wedding FAQ lists overnight valet at $50, or $55 for an oversized vehicle, in the Joseph Garage on Russell Street. Hotel LeVeque's offers page describes a Park and Stay package that includes valet for one vehicle and calls that valet a $50 value. We do not post invented nightly room rates. Ohio State weekends, commencements, and large conventions often tighten cancellation rules. If your dates sit near an event, prefer a more flexible rate even when that total is higher.

When should I book for Ohio State games, commencements, and downtown events?

Ohio Stadium is on the Ohio State campus. The Blackwell says it cannot provide its car service in and out of stadium or arena events because of the traffic. A Hilton travel article about Graduate describes an 8 minute drive from that High Street hotel to the stadium. That figure is the article's note, not a promise on a sold out Saturday. Commencement and recruiting weekends fill campus and the Short North. The Greater Columbus Convention Center is connected to Hilton Columbus Downtown, so a citywide meeting tightens that hotel and the blocks around it. Nationwide Arena and North Market are the walk the Hilton overview describes. Gallery Hop fills High Street on the first Saturday evening. The Joseph's wedding site describes 135 guestrooms and suites. The Blackwell describes 151. The Junto's FAQ says 198 guest rooms, including 31 suites. Hilton's 2019 opening release for Canopy described 167 rooms, including four suites. Hotel LeVeque describes 149 guest rooms, including six suites. Those houses can sell out while Hilton Columbus Downtown, which describes 1,000 guest rooms, still shows inventory. Compare the room type you actually want early on the official hotel site. Read the cancellation window before you pay. We do not invent nightly prices or a team schedule.

Do I need a car from CMH, or is the bus enough?

A Short North, downtown, or Arena District stay can work with walking, COTA buses, and rideshare for dinner, the North Market, and a German Village outing. There is no downtown rail from John Glenn Columbus International Airport. The airport's ground transportation page says rideshare pickup, including Uber and Lyft, is on the arrivals level. After you reserve the ride, go to level 1, cross the arrivals drive to the ground transportation area, and stand between the columns to the right. Taxis are on the ground transportation level. The same page points to COTA's trip planner and does not describe a train. COTA's fare page lists a $2 two hour pass for standard, frequent, and rush hour service, a $4.50 day pass, and fare capping at $4.50 a day or $62 a calendar month when you pay with the Transit app or a Smartcard. Children age 4 and under ride at no cost. The fare page says service changes come in January, May, and September, so confirm the line on the trip planner. The Junto's FAQ describes the airport as 10 miles from the hotel. Hilton Columbus Downtown's overview describes John Glenn as 8 miles away and says Rickenbacker is 17 miles away. Hilton Columbus at Easton's page describes the airport as four miles away. Those are each hotel's planning note. A car helps with airport luggage, Easton, Grandview, and a German Village evening if you do not want the bus, and it is a burden to park downtown on an event night. We do not publish live traffic times or a taxi fare. Book on the official hotel site when the hotel, room type, and cancellation terms already fit. The Graduate name remains. Book the Hilton page, not a retired checkout.
