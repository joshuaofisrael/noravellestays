Best luxury & boutique hotels in Columbus, Ohio

Columbus premium stays cluster in the Short North’s boutique scene, downtown full-service towers, and polished Easton-area hotels — a university and conference city with real design-hotel energy for weekends that deserve better than basic chains. Qualitative guidance for travelers planning a high-end trip — compare rates on partner sites when you are ready.

Best luxury neighborhoods for a splurge

Short North Arts District

The boutique sweet spot: galleries, dining, and lifestyle hotels for couples and design-minded leisure travelers.

Downtown / Arena District

Full-service and modern luxury for conventions, sports, and business-premium itineraries with walkable dining.

German Village edge

Historic character; look for carefully renovated inns and boutique properties when you want quiet evenings after downtown days.

Easton / Polaris upscale

Newer lifestyle brands and shopping-adjacent luxury when your plans sit on the north side.

Near CMH (premium)

Choose quieter full-service airport hotels for dawn departures rather than treating them as a Short North substitute.

Practical tips for a premium stay

  • Ohio State weekends, commencements, and major conventions move Short North and downtown rates — compare early for suites.
  • Short North walkability is the luxury product for many leisure trips; downtown wins when the arena or convention center is the center of gravity.
