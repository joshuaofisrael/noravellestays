Hotels in Nashville, Tennessee

Music City lodging spans loud Broadway blocks, walkable Midtown, and calmer East Nashville cottages-turned-inns. Qualitative guidance for US travelers — compare rates on partner sites when you are ready.

Neighborhoods at a glance

Downtown / Broadway

Best if nightlife and Lower Broadway are the trip. Expect noise, surge pricing on weekends, and easy rideshare access. Look for higher floors and rooms facing away from the street when comparing properties.

Midtown / Music Row

A middle ground: restaurants and venues without living on Broadway. Useful for visitors who want walkability plus quieter nights.

East Nashville

Indie restaurants and a neighborhood feel. Plan on rideshares downtown; good for longer stays and travelers who prefer local cafés over neon.

The Gulch

Compact, modern, and popular with weekend visitors who want dining within a short walk. Inventory skews newer and often pricier on peak weekends.

Near the airport (BNA)

Practical for dawn departures or late arrivals — see our Nashville Airport hotels page.

Practical tips

  • CMA Fest, NFL games, and big concerts can sell out central inventory — book earlier for those windows or stay Midtown/East with a clear rideshare budget.
  • Parking fees downtown add up; factor garage rates into your total when you compare partner offers.
