Best luxury & boutique hotels in Charleston, South Carolina

Charleston rewards a walkable Historic District boutique or five-star-caliber inn for anniversary and culinary weekends — Mount Pleasant resort-style properties are a deliberate premium Plan B, not a bargain strip. Qualitative guidance for travelers planning a high-end trip — compare rates on partner sites when you are ready.

Best luxury neighborhoods for a splurge

Historic District / peninsula boutiques

Gas-lantern streets, courtyard suites, and walkable dining. Prioritize quieter wings and interior courtyards when sleep matters.

Upper King / mid-peninsula

Still walkable with a slightly less tourist-core evening — strong for design-led renovations.

Mount Pleasant resort-caliber

More suite space, pools, and spa campuses with a known bridge commute.

West Ashley polished hotels

Farther from the core; use when you still want high-touch service.

Folly Beach luxury days

Beach-first splurges — weaker as a pure historic-district base.

Practical tips for a premium stay

  • Wedding season and peak spring tourism tighten peninsula luxury inventory — compare early.
