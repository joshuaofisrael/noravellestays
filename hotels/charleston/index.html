Hotels in Charleston, South Carolina

Charleston lodging decisions often come down to historic-district walkability versus bridge-crossing stays in Mount Pleasant or West Ashley. Qualitative guidance for US travelers — compare rates on partner sites when you are ready.

Editorial guidance only. We do not sell lodging, collect payment, or act as merchant of record. Prices and availability change — verify on the partner site before you book. See our Disclaimer and Affiliate Disclosure.

Neighborhoods at a glance

Historic District / Peninsula

Maximum walkability to markets, waterfront, and dining. Premium pricing and limited parking are common trade-offs.

Upper King / Midtown peninsula

Still walkable to many restaurants with a slightly less tourist-core feel on some blocks.

Mount Pleasant

More chain and resort-style inventory; you will cross the bridge for peninsula days. Good for families wanting space.

West Ashley

Often more affordable; plan drive or rideshare time into the historic core.

Folly Beach area

Beach-first trips — not ideal as a pure historic-district base without accepting commute time.

Practical tips

  • Wedding and spring tourist seasons tighten peninsula inventory — compare Mount Pleasant if dates are inflexible.
  • Humidity and walking distances matter; prioritize location over a slightly lower suburban rate if you dislike daily parking hunts.
