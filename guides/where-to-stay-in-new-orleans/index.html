Where to stay in New Orleans

Decide how close you want to be to the densest nightlife before you fall in love with a headline rate.

Editorial guidance only. We do not sell lodging, collect payment, or act as merchant of record. Prices and availability change — verify on the partner site before you book. See our Disclaimer and Affiliate Disclosure.

French Quarter

Walkable and iconic. Bourbon-adjacent blocks are not for light sleepers. Courtyard hotels can help.

Warehouse & CBD

Modern inventory and museum access with a short hop to the Quarter — a frequent sweet spot.

Garden District & Uptown

Streetcar charm and calmer evenings; great when the Quarter is for daytime only.

Festival reality

Mardi Gras and Jazz Fest rewrite availability citywide. Compare early and read cancellation terms carefully.
