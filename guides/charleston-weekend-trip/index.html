Charleston weekend trip

Two days in Charleston go furthest with a walkable peninsula base — or a deliberate bridge-crossing plan.

Editorial guidance only. We do not sell lodging, collect payment, or act as merchant of record. Prices and availability change — verify on the partner site before you book. See our Disclaimer and Affiliate Disclosure.

Friday arrival

Settle near your dinner reservation. Historic District lodging saves evening logistics; Mount Pleasant works if you accept bridge time.

Saturday

Morning market or waterfront walk, afternoon museum or plantation choice (pick one deep experience over racing both), evening Upper King dining.

Sunday

Brunch and a slower neighborhood stroll before departure. Factor church traffic and brunch waits into checkout timing.

Lodging tip

Wedding season and spring weekends tighten peninsula rooms — compare Charleston hotels early.
