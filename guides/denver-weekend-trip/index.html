Denver weekend trip

City museums and RiNo evenings pair well with a downtown or Cap Hill base — leave mountain summits for a longer trip unless you are acclimated.

Editorial guidance only. We do not sell lodging, collect payment, or act as merchant of record. Prices and availability change — verify on the partner site before you book. See our Disclaimer and Affiliate Disclosure.

Altitude first

Drink water, ease alcohol on night one, and keep day-one hikes modest if you flew in from sea level.

Where to sleep

LoDo/downtown for walkability; RiNo for brewery evenings; Cap Hill for a residential-urban mix. See Denver hotels.

Saturday

Art museum or Union Station morning, RiNo afternoon, early dinner before big-show crowds.

Sunday

Cherry Creek or a short foothills overlook — confirm weather and driving conditions in winter.
