Where to stay in Nashville: best places to stay by neighborhood

By Noravelle Stays Editorial. Updated October 2026.

The best places to stay in Nashville depend on how close you want to be to Lower Broadway. A first visit usually sleeps in the Gulch. Couples who want a landmark or a spa sleep at the Hermitage or in SoBro. Nightlife still should not mean a room on the honky tonk strip. Quiet luxury is Midtown, West End, or a house in East Nashville.

The Gulch

This is the best area to stay in Nashville for a first visit when you want restaurants in a short walk and you can treat Lower Broadway as an outing. The Gulch sits just south of downtown. Patio and rooftop sound are part of the product. A West End hotel is the better night if the morning has to be quiet.

Thompson Nashville

The official page is the Hyatt link. A Visit Music City reservations listing for a September 2026 event places Thompson Nashville, by Hyatt, at 401 11th Avenue South. The Gulch directory lists the same door. Choose it when the Gulch is the stay and a social roof is acceptable. We are not copying fees from those listings. Read the total on Hyatt.

W Nashville

This is the other Gulch hotel on the list. The Marriott overview was live for this update and describes the WET Deck and a fitness floor. The street number is on that Marriott page. Confirm it there before you book. It is a neighborhood deck, not a quiet West End night. The longer note is on the Nashville hotels page.

Downtown, off Lower Broadway

Lower Broadway is the honky tonk strip. That energy is why many people come, and it is why a luxury night usually sleeps a few blocks off it. These three hotels are downtown. None of them is a room over the loudest block. Ask which side the room faces.

The Hermitage Hotel

The official site was live and the page includes 231 6th Avenue North, at Union. It is the historic hotel nearer the State Capitol than the strip. The site’s own title uses star language. That is the hotel’s marketing, not a score we assigned. Ask for a quieter room if the trip is an anniversary.

Noelle

The official site lists 200 Fourth Avenue North. It is the downtown boutique on the Capitol side of the strip, close enough for one Broadway night and far enough that you should still ask which room faces the quieter side. It is not a Gulch restaurant walk and it is not a West End morning.

The Nash

The Opal Collection page titles the hotel as The Nash, formerly the Bobby Hotel, and the page text places it on Printers Alley. Use it when you want that alley and a walk toward Broadway. Ask which room sits off the roof if a rooftop is open on your dates. Read the current name on the site so an old Bobby Hotel listing does not confuse the booking.

SoBro and the river

SoBro, south of Broadway along Demonbreun and Korean Veterans Boulevard, is the best place to stay in Nashville when you want a newer hotel, a pool or a spa, and the Country Music Hall of Fame nearby. Broadway is still close. Ask for a higher floor or a room off the roof.

Four Seasons Hotel Nashville

The hotel’s own page describes a SoBro stay, a spa, and a seventh level pool and hot tub facing the Cumberland River and Riverfront Park. It also describes river view rooms toward the river and Nissan Stadium. Choose it for couples who want that full service base. We are not repeating a star claim from the hotel’s marketing as if it were our rating. Confirm the room and the fees on the site.

The Joseph, a Luxury Collection Hotel

The official site lists 401 Korean Veterans Boulevard. It is the SoBro hotel on that boulevard, walkable toward the hall of fame and toward Broadway. Ask whether the roof is open for your dates, because a rooftop can be seasonal. The room you want is on the hotel site, not in a rate box here.

1 Hotel Nashville

The hotel’s page lists the street address as 710 Demonbreun Street and describes a sustainable luxury hotel in downtown Nashville. It is the third SoBro name in this set, not a Gulch inn and not a West End campus hotel. Read pet rules and any fee on the official site. We do not copy awards onto this page.

Midtown, Music Row, and West End

This is the quiet luxury answer when one show is enough and the morning should not start on Broadway. Downtown is a short rideshare. You give up a river pool at the door.

Hutton Hotel

The official site lists 1808 West End Avenue. It is the West End hotel in this set, in the Midtown band rather than on Lower Broadway. Ask whether an event is in the building if sleep is the point. It is the calmer base for couples who still want one show downtown.

Loews Nashville Hotel at Vanderbilt Plaza

The hotel page title places it near Vanderbilt, and the page text includes 2100 West End Avenue. Use it for a campus visit, a medical appointment, or a family weekend that should not end on a honky tonk block. Hutton is a few blocks away. Broadway from either door is a ride.

East Nashville

East Nashville is across the Cumberland. This shortlist has one hotel there, so we are not adding a second name to fill the section.

Urban Cowboy

The official page includes 1603 Woodland Street. It is the house scale stay for Five Points dining. You will not walk home from a honky tonk. Stadium nights can still add bridge traffic. Ask which suite sits off the busiest side. A small house can sell through on a festival weekend while a SoBro hotel still shows rooms.

FAQ

Where should I stay in Nashville for the first time?

Stay in the Gulch if restaurants should be outside the door and Lower Broadway can be a rideshare. Thompson Nashville is the Hyatt hotel a Visit Music City reservations listing places at 401 11th Avenue South. W Nashville is the other Gulch hotel on this list. Its Marriott overview was live for this update. If you want a pool or a spa on the first night, sleep in SoBro instead, at Four Seasons Hotel Nashville, The Joseph, or 1 Hotel Nashville.

What are the best places to stay in Nashville for couples?

Choose a hotel that is not on the honky tonk strip. The Hermitage Hotel lists 231 6th Avenue North, nearer the State Capitol than Lower Broadway. Four Seasons Hotel Nashville describes a SoBro stay with a spa and a seventh level pool facing the river. Hutton Hotel lists 1808 West End Avenue. Ask for a quieter room, then confirm the rate on the official site.

Where should I stay in Nashville for nightlife?

Do not plan to sleep inside the loudest Lower Broadway block. Noelle lists 200 Fourth Avenue North. The Nash, formerly the Bobby Hotel, is the Printers Alley hotel on the Opal Collection site. The Hermitage Hotel at 231 6th Avenue North is a little farther from the strip. Broadway is a walk you plan, and you ask which room sits off the bar or the roof.

Where is the quiet luxury stay in Nashville?

Midtown and West End are calmer than Lower Broadway after dinner. Hutton Hotel is at 1808 West End Avenue. Loews Nashville Hotel at Vanderbilt Plaza is the full service hotel on that same West End corridor. Urban Cowboy, at 1603 Woodland Street, is the East Nashville house. Broadway from there is a rideshare, not a walk home.
