New York or Miami: comparing luxury hotels and suites

By Noravelle Stays Editorial. Last updated 27 October 2026.

Choose New York when the trip is neighborhoods you can walk, a park, and a suite with a separate living room. Choose Miami when the morning has to be the Atlantic, and accept a causeway if you sleep in Brickell or Surfside. Book the suite on the official hotel site, and confirm the view and the floor in writing.

How the two cities differ

The useful comparison is the job of the week, not a winner. New York luxury in this set is a walkable address: Midtown on Fifth Avenue, the Upper East Side by Central Park, or Downtown in Tribeca. You eat, walk, and come back to one building. Miami luxury, as our Miami hotel guide already maps it, splits sand at the door from a city hotel with the beach as a ride. South Beach and Mid Beach keep the Atlantic outside. Surfside slows that morning down. Brickell puts dining and the bay first.

Use the same guest tests in both cities. The checklist is how to choose a luxury hotel. The edit behind a city short list is how we pick luxury hotels. This page does not publish rates, star scores, or a ranking.

How to book a suite

Book on the official hotel website. Do not start from a comparison button. Affiliate comparison on Noravelle Stays is not open, and this site does not take payment.

Read the suite category name on the hotel site before you fall for the word suite. Email the hotel and ask three things in one note: what the category includes, which floor it is on, and what the window faces. Ask for the reply in writing, and keep it with the reservation. If the name on the booking screen is not the name in the email, stop and reconcile them.

In New York, the separate living room is the test that matters. In Miami, also ask whether you are buying an oceanfront room, a suite, or a bungalow, and whether the beach is at the door or a causeway away. Read fees and the cancellation rule on that same official page. A package is real only when the hotel describes what it includes. We do not sell packages.

New York picks

These four hotels were open on their official sites when we read those sites on 26 October 2026. Each site offers a way to reserve or inquire. A name here is not a rate and not a promise about a view.

  • Aman New York is in the Crown Building at 730 Fifth Avenue, at Fifth Avenue and 57th Street in Midtown. The official site describes suites and homes, plus a spa. Ask which category has a separate living room.
  • The Carlyle, A Rosewood Hotel is at 35 East 76th Street. The official site presents an Upper East Side hotel with suites and guestrooms, and it names Bemelmans Bar and Café Carlyle. Ask for a room away from the entertainment rooms if sleep is the point.
  • Four Seasons Hotel New York Downtown is at 27 Barclay Street. The official site describes a Downtown hotel in Tribeca and lists named suites. Read that list so a large bedroom is not mistaken for a separate living room.
  • The Pierre, A Taj Hotel is at 2 East 61st Street at Fifth Avenue. The official site places it on the Upper East Side, across from Central Park, and describes suites, including a Premier Pierre Suite. Confirm the floor and the park view in writing.

For the Rockefeller Center tree, use the Rockefeller tree lighting guide. Aman New York and The Pierre are the two hotels above that sit on Fifth Avenue. That guide also uses Baccarat Hotel New York and The St. Regis New York, which are closer to the plaza. The Carlyle and Four Seasons Hotel New York Downtown stay on this page for an Upper East Side stay and a Tribeca stay. The tree guide leaves them off because those addresses are not a walk to Rockefeller Center.

Miami picks

These four are already on the Miami hotel guide. This page does not add new claims about them. Open the guide for the neighborhood essay, then the official site for the room. Mandarin Oriental, Miami is not a pick. That official page says the hotel bid farewell on 31 May 2025 and describes a reimagined hotel targeted for 2030.

  • The Setai Miami Beach, 2001 Collins Avenue, is the South Beach oceanfront hotel in our guide. The guide already says the courtyard is part of the night. Ask for a quieter room if sleep matters.
  • Faena Hotel Miami Beach, 3201 Collins Avenue, is the Mid Beach pick, north of the densest Ocean Drive blocks.
  • Four Seasons Hotel Miami, 1435 Brickell Avenue, is the Brickell hotel. The guide says rooms face the city or the bay, and the beach is a ride.
  • Four Seasons Hotel at The Surf Club, 9011 Collins Avenue in Surfside, is the slower ocean morning. The guide says South Beach is a ride.
