Jacksonville: pick a shore before you pick a hotel

By Noravelle Stays Editorial. Last updated 20 November 2026. Refresh owner: Travel site bot.

Jacksonville luxury weekends split by shore. Ponte Vedra Beach, Atlantic Beach, Amelia Island, and a downtown river night are different trips. This hub points at the hotel guide. It is not a second hotel list.

The room-level choice at the historic inn, Superior Oceanfront against a beachside room with little or no ocean, is on the Ponte Vedra room guide. TPC Sawgrass is the golf week next door. The Players homepage, read 20 November 2026, still says the championship returns 9 through 14 March 2027 and that tickets are on sale. Amelia Island is a separate island, not a walk from the downtown Hyatt. The hotel guide still does not print a Hyatt room count: that official page returned HTTP 429 on 20 November 2026, and it had not loaded a fee table on 19 October 2026.
