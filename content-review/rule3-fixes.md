# Rule 3 image fixes

Checked 2026-10-27. Every previous manifest row was HOLD. The files had no licence URL, source page, creator contract, or embedded copyright metadata. Page credit calling them original Noravelle artwork was not a licence, so none of those bytes were kept.

A replacement is a Wikimedia Commons file whose licence permits commercial use. The caption names the real subject. A city, street, or beach photograph is not presented as a named hotel. Generic photographs say they are illustrative and are not a hotel reviewed on this site. Hotel cards that had mood art now have no photograph, because a picture of a different place must not sit in that card.
